The short answer

What PPC management services include.

PPC management is the ongoing work of running pay-per-click advertising so that it produces customers at a cost the business can afford. That means building and structuring campaigns on Google Ads and other platforms, choosing keywords and audiences, writing ads, setting bids and budgets, tracking conversions properly, and making changes every week based on what the numbers show.

PPC management services are that work done for you by a specialist team. The difference between a managed account and an unmanaged one is rarely the launch. It is the weekly attention afterwards: the search terms that waste money, the ad that has stopped working, the budget that should move to the campaign that is finding customers. If you want the full picture before you talk to anyone, when and how to outsource PPC management walks through it.

When it is time

Signs your PPC account needs managing.

  • Cost per lead or cost per sale has crept up and nobody can say why.
  • The search terms report has not been opened in weeks. Every account collects irrelevant searches; unmanaged ones keep paying for them.
  • Performance Max or Smart Bidding was switched on and no one is sure what it is optimising for.
  • Conversions in Google Ads do not match your CRM or store. If the numbers disagree, bidding is learning from the wrong signal.
  • The same ads have run for months. Ads tire, and competitors change their offers.
  • Budget runs out by mid-afternoon, or never gets spent at all.
  • The person running PPC has three other jobs. Good PPC management needs a little attention most days, not a big session once a month.

Pricing models

How PPC management services are priced.

Agencies price PPC management in three ways. A percentage of ad spend is common and simple, but it pays the agency more when your budget rises, whether or not results do. A fixed monthly fee stays the same as spend scales, so the advice to cut a budget costs the agency nothing. A performance fee ties payment to leads or sales, which sounds fair and usually comes with conditions that are hard to meet.

We charge a fixed monthly fee, month to month, with no lock-in and never a percentage of ad spend. The fee depends on the number of platforms and how much work the account needs, so we quote after the free audit and say the number on the first call. Before you sign

How to choose a PPC management company.

  • The ad accounts stay in your name. A company that wants to own your Google Ads account is building leverage, not a service.
  • A written audit before a proposal. Anyone who quotes without looking at the account is guessing.
  • No guaranteed results before they have seen your margins. A promise made blind is a sales line.
  • Reporting in cost per customer. Clicks, impressions and click-through rate are inputs, not results.
  • A named person on your account. Ask who does the work day to day, and who covers when they are away.
  • Month to month, or a short first term. A good PPC management company keeps clients with results, not contracts.
