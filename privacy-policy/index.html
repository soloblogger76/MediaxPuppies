Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: 14 July 2026 · Media Puppies, Delhi NCR, India

1. Who we are

Media Puppies ("we", "us") is a performance marketing and lead generation agency based in Delhi NCR, India. This page explains what data we collect through mediapuppies.com, how we use it, and your rights. For any questions, contact us at info@mediapuppies.com.

2. Information we collect

When you submit our enquiry form, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Business type, business name / website, and city
  • Approximate monthly marketing budget
  • WhatsApp / phone number

We also use standard analytics and advertising tools (such as Meta Pixel and Google tags) that collect device and usage information through cookies to measure ad performance and show relevant ads. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry on WhatsApp or by phone with a proposed lead plan
  • To assess whether our services fit your business
  • To measure and improve our own advertising campaigns

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We do not add you to bulk marketing lists. Your details are shared only with the service providers we use to run this website (form processing, analytics), who are bound to handle them securely.

4. Data retention & your rights

We keep enquiry data only as long as needed to serve you. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time — email us at info@mediapuppies.com and we will action it within 7 working days.

5. Terms of service

  • No guaranteed outcomes: advertising results vary by industry, offer, budget and market. We commit to transparent reporting and daily campaign management — not to a fixed number of leads or customers.
  • Ad spend: your ad budget is paid by you directly to Meta/Google from your own ad account. Our management fee is separate.
  • Engagement: services are month-to-month unless agreed otherwise in writing. Either party may end the engagement with notice before the next billing cycle.
  • Compliance: you are responsible for the accuracy and legality of claims about your own products and services used in ad creatives we prepare with you.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the current version.
