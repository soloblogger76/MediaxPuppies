Meta Ads Creative Designer

The role

What you will do.

  • Design static and carousel ads in every Meta placement size: feed, Stories and Reels
  • Edit short video ads and UGC-style ads where the first three seconds earn the next ten
  • Make several variations of each winning idea: new hooks, new openings, new formats
  • Work with the media buyers who run the ads, read the results with them, and turn the data into the next round of concepts
  • Keep a tidy creative library with names that match the ad accounts, so every test can be traced
About you

What we look for.

  • A portfolio of ads you made for Facebook and Instagram. Real ads beat polished mock-ups
  • Confident in Figma or Photoshop, and in a video editor such as Premiere Pro, After Effects or CapCut
  • You know what a hook is, why ads fatigue, and how hook rate and thumb-stop differ from a nice design
  • You can take feedback based on numbers without taking it personally
  • Written English good enough to work with clients in the US, UK and Australia
Good to have

Not required, but useful.

  • Experience with UGC creators: briefing them, editing their raw footage, adding captions
  • Motion design for ad openings
  • Familiarity with the Meta Ad Library and how competitors in a category advertise
How hiring works

Three steps.

  • You apply with your portfolio and LinkedIn. A person reads every application
  • A 15-minute call about your work
  • A short creative task based on a real brief, then a decision. We reply either way
