Who we are

A paid media team that writes from live accounts.

Media Puppies is a performance marketing team based in Noida, India. We have spent eight years running paid media and have managed more than $10M in ad spend across Google, Meta and LinkedIn. Every article on this blog comes from that work: the same checks, formulas and mistakes we deal with in client accounts each week.

We write for business owners, marketing leads and agencies who want to understand what is happening inside their ad accounts, and for agencies that resell paid media under their own brand.

What we write about

Topics we cover.

  • PPC management and Google Ads: structure, search terms, bidding, Performance Max, audits
  • Meta ads: creative testing, Advantage+, Conversions API
  • LinkedIn ads and B2B lead generation
  • White label PPC and working with agency partners
  • Tracking, attribution and reporting, plus our free marketing calculators
How we write

Our editorial standards.

  • Written by practitioners who run ad accounts, then checked against platform documentation.
  • No invented statistics or client results. Worked examples use clearly hypothetical numbers.
  • No prices or fees in articles. We discuss pricing models, and say our own fee on the first call.
  • Every article shows when it was published and when it was last updated.
  • Spotted an error? Email info@mediapuppies.com and we will fix it.
