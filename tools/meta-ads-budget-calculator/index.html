How this works

The arithmetic is trivial — leads multiplied by cost per lead. The part that matters is the third number.

Meta's delivery system needs roughly 50 conversions per ad set per week before it leaves what it calls the learning phase. Below that, it does not have enough signal to find the right people, so cost per lead stays high and swings wildly from day to day. This is the single most common reason a small Indian ad account underperforms: the budget is spread across four ad sets, none of which ever gets enough data.

What to do if the number is too low

  • Merge ad sets. One ad set at ₹1,200 a day beats four at ₹300.
  • Optimise for an earlier event. If purchases are too rare, optimise for add-to-cart or lead, then move down the funnel once volume allows.
  • Raise the budget, if the customer value supports it — check that with the CAC calculator.
  • Accept slower learning and do not touch the campaign for three weeks. Every edit restarts the learning phase.

A note on cost per lead

If you have never run ads, you do not know your cost per lead and nobody can tell you accurately — it depends on your offer, city, industry and creative. Budget for a first month that buys you the answer rather than the result.

Questions

What is the learning phase?+

The period where Meta is still working out who to show your ad to. Delivery is unstable and cost per result is usually higher than it will settle at. It ends after roughly 50 optimisation events in a rolling seven-day window, per ad set.

Does editing a campaign restart learning?+

Significant edits do — budget changes above about 20%, changing the optimisation event, editing targeting or swapping creative. Small budget nudges usually do not. This is why "leave it alone for a week" is real advice rather than laziness.

Should the budget be daily or lifetime?+

Daily for ongoing lead generation, because it is predictable. Lifetime suits a campaign with a fixed end date, like a festive offer.

Is a higher budget always better?+

No. Spending beyond what your audience size and sales capacity support raises frequency and cost per lead. Scale in steps of roughly 20% and watch cost per lead after each one.
