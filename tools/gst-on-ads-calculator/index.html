The part most advertisers miss

Meta and Google bill Indian advertisers from a foreign entity and the invoice usually shows no GST. Many people read that as “no tax on ad spend”. It is not — it is an import of service, and a registered recipient is expected to self-assess IGST on it under the reverse charge mechanism, then claim the same amount back as input credit.

For a registered business with taxable output that nets to zero and is purely cash flow and compliance. For a business that is unregistered, or whose output is exempt, it does not net to zero.

Where the cost actually lands

SituationWhat GST does to you
Registered, fully taxable outputA timing difference — pay now, credit later.
Registered, partly exemptPart of the credit is lost and becomes real cost.
Registered, exempt outputThe whole 18% sticks.
Not registeredSuppliers’ GST is unrecoverable. No reverse-charge obligation, but no credit either.

Why this changes your ROAS target

If the tax is real cost rather than timing, your break-even moves. A brand paying an unrecoverable 18% on agency fees and tools is running a higher cost base than the one it modelled. Put the “true marketing cost” figure into the break-even ROAS calculator rather than the headline spend.

This is a planning tool, not tax advice. Rates, reverse-charge treatment and credit eligibility depend on your registration, your state and what you supply. Confirm the specifics with your CA before filing anything.

Questions

Do Meta and Google charge GST on Indian ad accounts?+

Typically the invoice comes from a foreign entity with no Indian GST, which makes it an import of service, and a registered recipient self-assesses IGST under reverse charge. Billing arrangements change, so check your own invoice rather than assuming.

Can I claim input credit on advertising?+

Generally yes where the spend is for business and you hold a valid invoice reflected in your returns. Eligibility depends on your circumstances — check with your CA.

What is the GST rate on advertising?+

18% is the standard rate on advertising and marketing services in India.

Should a small brand register just for this?+

That depends on far more than advertising — turnover thresholds, who your customers are, and whether they need a tax invoice. This shows what the advertising side of the decision is worth.
