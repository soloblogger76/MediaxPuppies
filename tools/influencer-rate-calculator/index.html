Follower count is the least useful number

It is the one every quote is built on and the one easiest to buy. Reach and engagement are harder to fake and tell you what you are actually purchasing. Always ask for a screenshot of the last 30 days of insights, not a single post.

The conversation the last number forces

Most influencer deals are agreed without anyone working out how many sales would be needed to justify them. Doing that arithmetic before you negotiate changes the discussion entirely — either the price comes down, or both sides agree openly that this is awareness spend and should be judged on something other than orders.

What this cannot tell you

Fit. A creator with weaker numbers whose audience genuinely trusts them on your category will outperform a larger one who posts a different brand every day. Check how many paid posts they ran last month before you check anything here.

Compare the cost per 1,000 reached against your own paid social CPM using the media math calculator. If the creator costs several times more, the premium has to be justified by trust, not reach.

Questions

What engagement rate is good in India?+

It varies hugely by size and category, and smaller accounts normally show higher rates. Compare creators against each other within the same size band rather than against a universal benchmark.

How do I spot bought followers?+

Low reach relative to followers is the clearest signal, along with comments that are generic, follower spikes with no matching content, and an audience geography that does not match the creator.

Should I pay per post or on performance?+

Affiliate or hybrid deals shift risk to the creator and most established ones will decline. A reasonable middle ground is a lower flat fee plus a per-order bonus.

Does this work for YouTube and reels?+

Yes. Use views in place of reach, and likes plus comments for engagement.
