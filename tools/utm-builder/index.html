The convention that saves your reports

UTM values are case sensitive. Facebook, facebook and FaceBook become three separate rows in your analytics, and six months later nobody can total them. This tool lowercases everything and converts spaces to hyphens for exactly that reason.

Agree the vocabulary once and write it down. Most teams need only a handful of mediums: cpc, email, organic-social, paid-social, referral, affiliate. If a new medium appears every month, nobody is following a convention.

Where not to use UTMs

  • On internal links. Tagging a link from your own homepage to your own pricing page restarts the session and destroys the original attribution. This is the single most damaging UTM mistake.
  • Never put personal data in a UTM. The URL ends up in analytics, server logs and referrer headers.
  • Be careful with paid search auto-tagging. Google Ads adds gclid automatically; adding manual UTMs on top can override it. Pick one.

Questions

Do UTM parameters hurt SEO?+

Not if your pages have self-referencing canonical tags, which consolidate the tagged versions back to the clean URL. Without canonicals you can end up with many indexable versions of one page.

Are source, medium and campaign required?+

Analytics tools need all three to group traffic usefully. Content and term are optional.

Can I shorten the resulting link?+

Yes — a shortener preserves the parameters through the redirect. Use one for print, QR codes or SMS where the long URL is unwieldy.

Why does my traffic show as direct?+

Usually a missing or mistyped medium, an internal link that was tagged, or a redirect that strips the query string.
