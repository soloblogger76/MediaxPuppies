Pest control Google Ads cost in 2026: what a click costs in the US, UK, Australia and Canada

We took 200 pest control keywords from Ahrefs across four countries, removed the searches that are not someone hiring, and compared what is left. The US costs most, commercial searches cost most inside it, and the other three markets are mostly city searches.

We wanted a plain answer to a question pest control owners and the agencies that run their accounts keep asking: what does a click cost on pest control Google Ads in the US, the UK, Australia and Canada? We took the 200 pest control keywords we pulled from Ahrefs on 17 November 2026, 50 per country, and kept only the searches where someone is looking to hire.

The short answer: on Ahrefs estimates, the median buyer-intent pest control click cost $6 in the US, $3.50 in Australia, $3 in Canada and $2 in the UK. Commercial searches cost the most in the US, at $13 to $18. Semrush puts US prices much higher, with a median of $14.84, so read every figure here as a tool estimate and compare countries within one tool. The volume-weighted average sits above the median in the US, the UK and Australia. That is because the biggest searches are also among the dearer ones. "Pest control" itself, at 253,000 US searches a month, was $8, and "pest control near me" was $9. The US gap surprised us less than its size did. We expected the US to lead. We did not expect the UK median to be a third of it. Every price here is in US dollars, as Ahrefs shows them, so convert before you plan in pounds or local dollars.

Commercial searches are the dearest PPC for pest control

The two most expensive US buyer searches in the sample were commercial: "commercial pest control near me" at $18 and "commercial pest control" at $13. Their median, $15.50, was 2.58 times the $6 median of the other US buyer searches. The UK showed a similar gap, though it rests on one keyword: "commercial pest control" at $5 against a $2 median for the rest.

Canada had the most commercial keywords, 5, and a smaller gap of 1.33 times ($4 against $3). Australia's 2 commercial keywords came in at 1.29 times. Our view: commercial contracts are worth more per customer than one-off home visits, which fits a higher bid, and in the US it is the clearest case for a separate campaign with its own budget.

Brand names fill the US list; cities fill the rest

The shape of the four top 50 lists was the second surprise. In the US, 20 of the 50 highest-volume pest control searches were the names of other firms, such as Orkin, Terminix and Aptive. Their median CPC was $2.50, well under the US buyer median. We left them out of every buyer figure on this page, because someone searching for a named company is usually trying to reach that company. The US list held no city searches at all.

In the other three countries, city and suburb searches made up most of the buyer rows: 26 in the UK, 25 in Australia and 19 in Canada. They also cost more than general service searches. The city median was $4.50 in Australia against $3 for general searches, $3.50 in Canada against $2, and $2.50 in the UK against $2.

Emergency and same-day searches, from Semrush

Ahrefs' top 50 lists held almost no emergency searches: one in the UK, "emergency pest control" at $4, and none in the other three. For that cut we turned to the 17 US pest control keywords we priced in Semrush on 3 November 2026. Emergency and same-day searches had a median of $24.41, 1.98 times the $12.32 median of the other 15. "Same day pest control" was the dearest at $27.91.

Semrush prices US pest control much higher than Ahrefs. Its median across the 17 keywords was $14.84. On the 3 keywords both tools priced, the gap was consistent:

Our view: use Semrush or Ahrefs to rank keywords and compare countries, and use Google Keyword Planner for your exact service area before you set a budget.

What we would do with pest control Google Ads

  • Split commercial from residential. In the US, commercial searches ran at 2.58 times the rest. They deserve their own campaign, budget and landing page, so the dearer clicks are not mixed with home visits.
  • Build city campaigns outside the US. In the UK, Australia and Canada, most priced buyer searches named a city or suburb, and those cost more than general searches. Location targeting alone will not catch the price difference; separate ad groups make it visible.
  • Exclude other firms' brand names unless you mean to bid on them. 20 of the top 50 US searches were competitor names. Our view: most of those searchers want that company, so add them as negatives and check your search terms report every week. Our negative keywords audit covers how.
  • Keep emergency and same-day terms apart. On Semrush's US figures they cost nearly twice the rest, so they need their own bids and hours.
  • Weigh Local Services Ads in the US. Pest control is one of the US Local Services Ads categories, priced per lead. Our Local Services Ads guide explains how it sits next to search ads.

The shared keyword rule in that study flagged only "pest control jobs" as a non-buyer search. We read all 200 rows by eye and set 46 more to non-buyer in this study only: 36 brand names of other pest control firms (for example Orkin, Terminix, Rentokil, Flick and Avayda, including "reviews" searches), the RuneScape minigame "pest control osrs", DIY and how-to searches, and one price question. Keyword types are set by a written rule, first match wins: emergency words, then "near me", then a list of city and suburb names, then a named pest, then commercial, hotel or apartment wording, then everything else. Questions about pest control Google Ads and PPC costs

How much do pest control Google Ads cost per click?

On Ahrefs estimates of 17 November 2026, the median buyer-intent pest control click was $6 in the US, $3.50 in Australia, $3 in Canada and $2 in the UK, all in US dollars. Semrush puts the US higher, with a median of $14.84 across 17 keywords on 3 November 2026.

Which pest control keywords cost the most?

In the US, commercial searches: "commercial pest control near me" was $18 and "commercial pest control" $13 on Ahrefs. In Semrush, "same day pest control" was the dearest at $27.91. Outside the US, city and suburb searches such as "pest control parramatta" ($8) and "pest control hammersmith" ($7) led.

Is PPC for pest control cheaper in the UK than the US?

On our data, yes. The UK buyer median was $2 against $6 in the US, 33.33% of the US figure. On the 7 keywords found in all four countries' lists, the median UK price was 25% of the US price.

Should a pest control company bid on competitor names?

20 of the 50 highest-volume US pest control searches were other firms' names, priced at a median of $2.50. Our view: most of those people want that company, so we add brand names as negatives unless there is a clear reason to bid on them.

Are these real auction prices?

They are Ahrefs and Semrush estimates, each from the tool's own model, and neither tool sees Google's auctions. Your account's price depends on your bids, ads, landing page and service area, so check Google Keyword Planner for your locations and start with a small test.
