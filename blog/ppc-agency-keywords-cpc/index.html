PPC agency CPC in 2026: what agencies pay per click for their own clients in the US, UK, Canada and Australia

We priced the top 50 "ppc agency" searches in four countries with Ahrefs. The UK had the dearest median of any trade in our wider study, and the US split into two price tiers that sit twenty times apart.

Key findings

  • On Ahrefs estimates of 17 November 2026, the median buyer search containing "ppc agency" cost $12 a click in the UK, $7.50 in Australia, $5 in Canada and $3.50 in the US. 100 clicks at the median: $1,200, $750, $500 and $350.
  • The UK agency median was the highest of all 36 country and trade medians in our wider CPC study, and 2.0 times the UK locksmith median.
  • The US splits in two. Searches that name a niche, a business model, white label or a city had a median of $7 (23 keywords); generic wordings such as "ppc marketing agency" had a median of $0.35 (18 keywords), 20.0 times cheaper.
  • The single word pair "ppc agency" cost $20 in the US and $25 in the UK. In the US that is 6.67 times the median of the other 41 priced agency searches.
  • Most agency searches in Australia and Canada are too rare for Ahrefs to price: 68% of the Australian top 50 and 66% of the Canadian top 50 had no CPC, against 10% in the US.

Agencies spend their days pricing other people's clicks. We wanted the price of their own. From the Ahrefs pull behind our country CPC study we took the top 50 searches containing "ppc agency" in the US, the UK, Australia and Canada, 200 rows, and kept the 115 that carry a price and come from someone looking for a Google Ads agency. All figures are Ahrefs estimates in US dollars, collected on 17 November 2026.

The short answer on PPC agency CPC: the median priced agency search cost $12 a click in the UK, $7.50 in Australia, $5 in Canada and $3.50 in the US. The US median hides a split. Searches that name a niche, a city or white label had a median of $7, while vague wordings like "ppc marketing agency" sat at $0.35. The two plain words "ppc agency" cost $20 in the US and $25 in the UK.

We run white label PPC for agencies in those four countries, so we have an interest here. We have kept our own accounts and clients out of it. Every number below is from the keyword tools, with the sample size beside it.

Look at the US volume-weighted figure. At $10.78 it is three times the median, because the one search with real volume, "ppc agency" at 20,000 a month, is priced at $20 and carries 37.21% of the US buyer volume in the list. A US agency bidding on the obvious term pays the high number. The cheap median comes from the long tail.

Next to the trades in the parent study, agency clicks are dear. The UK agency median of $12 was the highest of the 36 country and trade medians we published, twice the UK locksmith figure of $6 and three times the UK plumber figure of $4. In the parent study's own medians, agency searches ranked 1st of 9 trades in the UK, 2nd in Australia and Canada (behind plumbers) and 3rd in the US. In the UK, Australia and Canada the agency median matched or beat every trade except plumbers, and in the UK it beat plumbers too.

Trade niches are where many agencies start; our guide to plumbing PPC shows what one of those accounts looks like.

The US has two price tiers

This was the result we did not expect. Of the 42 priced US agency searches, 19 (45.24%) cost under $1.50 a click, and those carried 44.65% of the buyer search volume. Another 12 (28.57%) cost $10 or more. Very little sat in between.

The cheap US searches are mostly rewordings of the head term. "ppc marketing agency" (4,200 searches a month) and "google ppc agency" (1,200) were priced at $0.30, "ppc agency near me" (2,100) at $0.30, and "ppc management agency" (3,200) at $1. Only the UK comes near that floor, with "ppc marketing agency" and "ppc management agency" both at $0.30.

Our view on why: a CPC estimate reflects who has bid on a phrase lately. Agencies that sell PPC tend to bid on the term they would type themselves and on their niche. The rewordings get fewer bidders. We would treat the $0.30 figures as a reason to test those phrases with a tight negative list, and as no promise that a lead from them will be cheap.

Which agency searches cost most

In the US, searches that name the client's industry were the dearest group, with a median of $11 across 8 keywords. "dental ppc agency" and "dentist ppc agency" were $25 each, "hvac ppc agency" and "plumber ppc agency" $11 each. Business models came close behind at $10: "saas ppc agency" at $20 and "shopify ppc agency" at $19, with "ecommerce ppc agency" at $1 and "b2b ppc agency" at $0.80.

The legal rows show how loose single estimates can be. "lawyer ppc agency" was $75, while "law firm ppc agency" was $0.20 and "ppc agency for law firms" $1.70. The same buyer types all three. The dearest US row of all, "multilingual ppc agency" at $90, has 250 searches a month. We report these as Ahrefs shows them and would not plan a budget on any one of them.

The head term behaves differently by market. In the US and the UK it is among the dearest searches in the list, and 100 clicks on it alone come to $2,000 and $2,500. In Canada "ppc agency" was $4, and 12 of the other 15 priced Canadian agency searches cost more.

We also set aside 7 searches for agencies that run other platforms' ads. They were not cheap either: "amazon ppc agency" was $20 in the US and $17 in the UK, "facebook ppc agency" $16 in the US and "bing ppc agency" $17 in the UK.

City searches and the UK premium

The UK list is mostly place names: 21 of its 41 priced searches. Their median was $12. The dearest were "ppc agency milton keynes" at $25, "ppc agency bristol" at $20 (1,800 searches a month), "best ppc agency london" at $19, and Manchester and Sheffield at $18 each. The plain "ppc agency london", with 1,300 searches, was the cheapest UK place search at $5.

US city searches were lower, a median of $7 across 10, led by "atlanta ppc agency" at $16 and "denver ppc agency" at $9. New York and Los Angeles were $4 each.

On the 16 keywords priced in both the US and the UK, the UK was dearer on 9 and the US on 5, and the median UK price was 125% of the US one. The widest gaps were on the cheap US rewordings:

White label searches

We checked white label searches because agencies are also each other's buyers. In the US, "white label ppc agency" (450 searches a month) was priced at $17, which is 85% of the price of "ppc agency". In Canada it was $9, the second dearest agency search in the country. The Australian version had 200 searches and no price.

Semrush tells a different story. In our US Semrush data from 3 November 2026, "white label ppc" was $14.28 against $46.56 for "ppc agency", or 30.67%. The two tools agree that white label clicks cost real money and disagree on whether they are cheaper than the head term. Both are single keywords, so we hold this loosely. Our white label PPC economics study covers the Semrush side in more depth.

Ahrefs vs Semrush on "ppc agency"

Only one keyword sits in both data sets with the same wording. Semrush priced US "ppc agency" at $46.56; Ahrefs priced it at $20, or 42.96% of Semrush. That gap matches what we found across trades in the parent study, where Ahrefs usually came in well under Semrush.

Several of the dearest agency searches are outside the Ahrefs pull because they do not contain "ppc agency". Semrush US put "google ads management" at $228.10, "ppc management" at $95.27 and "google ads agency" at $45.44. Those figures are in our B2B PPC cost per click study, and we have not re-analysed them here.

Australia and Canada are thin markets

In Australia 34 of the top 50 agency searches had no CPC, and in Canada 33. Most of those are suburb and city searches with 80 to 150 searches a month. Ahrefs needs bidding history to estimate a price, so a blank usually means few advertisers. The priced Australian searches were dear, a median of $7.50, led by "google ppc agency" at $16 and "ppc agency sydney" at $13. Canada's dearest was "google ppc agency" at $20 on 100 searches.

With 16 priced keywords in each country, we would read these medians as a range and confirm them in Keyword Planner before setting a budget.

What this means for an agency's own Google Ads

The client acquisition side of an agency is expensive on Google. In the UK the median priced agency search costs more than any trade we have measured, and in the US the searches that signal a ready buyer (a niche, a city, white label) cost $7 or more at the median. Our view is that this is one reason many agencies keep sales in house and hand fulfilment to a partner: each won client carries a real click bill, and the margin has to cover it. We say that as a white label provider, so weigh it accordingly.

  • Bid your niche only if you serve it. "dental ppc agency" at $25 is worth it to an agency with dental results to show. Our guide to choosing a Google Ads agency lists what those buyers check.
  • Test the cheap rewordings in the US. A separate ad group for "ppc marketing agency", "ppc management agency" and similar, with exact match and tight negatives, costs little to learn from.
  • Price the UK on its own. At 342.86% of the US median, a UK campaign needs its own budget line.
  • Do not budget on one keyword. The legal rows above swing from $0.20 to $75. Plan from group medians and check Keyword Planner.
  • Know your other-platform rows. Amazon and Facebook agency searches cost $16 to $20 in the US. Add them as negatives if you only run Google.

These rows are the "ppc agency" part of our Google Ads CPC by country study. On 17 November 2026 we opened the Ahrefs "Matching terms" report for "ppc agency" in the US, UK, Australian and Canadian databases and kept the top 50 keywords by monthly search volume. Ahrefs shows CPC in US dollars for every country. 79 rows had no CPC and are left out of every median.

We read all 200 rows by eye. None named a specific agency, and none were job, course, salary or definition searches. We took 8 rows out of the medians: "ppc agency london" in the US database, and 7 searches for Amazon, Facebook or Bing agencies, which are buyers of other platforms' ads. Those 7 are reported on their own. The list is in compile.py, so the US median here ($3.50) differs from the parent study ($4).

Each keyword sits in one group, first match wins: the head term "ppc agency", white label, industry niche (dental, lawyer, law firm, hvac, plumber, hotel, healthcare and similar), business model (ecommerce, Shopify, SaaS, B2B), near me, city or country name, best or top, management or services, and other wording. "Specific" searches are the niche, white label, business model and place groups. "Generic wording" is the rest apart from the head term.

What the data cannot price. "Matching terms" only returns searches that contain both words, so "google ads agency", "ppc management services" and price questions are outside it, and no cost question made any country's top 50. For those we point to the Semrush US figures in our earlier studies.

Limits. These are Ahrefs and Semrush model estimates. Neither is Google Keyword Planner and neither is an auction price. Australia and Canada have 16 priced keywords each, and several groups hold one keyword, so we give n beside each median. Some single prices contradict each other, which we read as noise on small searches.

Questions about PPC agency CPC and agency keywords

What is the average PPC agency CPC?

On Ahrefs estimates of 17 November 2026, the median priced search containing "ppc agency" cost $12 a click in the UK, $7.50 in Australia, $5 in Canada and $3.50 in the US. The two words "ppc agency" on their own cost $20 in the US and $25 in the UK.

Why are UK agency clicks so expensive?

We can only describe it from the data. The UK median was 342.86% of the US median, and UK city searches such as Bristol, Manchester and Milton Keynes sat at $18 to $25. The UK was dearer than the US on 9 of 16 keywords priced in both.

Which agency keywords cost the most?

In the US, searches naming an industry or business model: "lawyer ppc agency" at $75, "dental ppc agency" at $25 and "saas ppc agency" at $20. Generic wordings such as "ppc marketing agency" cost $0.30. Single estimates swing a lot, so plan from group medians.

How much does a white label PPC click cost?

Ahrefs priced "white label ppc agency" at $17 in the US and $9 in Canada. Semrush put the shorter "white label ppc" at $14.28 in the US. Both are single-keyword estimates.

Are Ahrefs and Semrush CPCs the same for agency keywords?

No. For US "ppc agency", Semrush said $46.56 and Ahrefs $20, which is 42.96% of the Semrush figure. Neither is an auction price, so treat both as a range and check Google Keyword Planner.
