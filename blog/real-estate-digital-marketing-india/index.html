Real estate digital marketing in India: the full funnel, from portal to site visit

Most property marketing plans list channels. The ones that sell units manage the path from the first enquiry to the site visit, because that is where most money is lost.

Media Puppies · 23 October 2026

Real estate digital marketing in India is the set of online channels a developer or broker uses to find buyers and move them to a site visit: property portals, Google search, Meta ads, the project website, SEO, WhatsApp and the CRM that ties them together. No single channel sells a flat. The sale comes from how well these pieces hand a buyer from one step to the next.

This guide looks at real estate digital marketing as one funnel, with the job each channel does and the numbers that show whether the whole thing is working.

How the funnel fits together

  1. Attract. Meta and portals create awareness for a launch; Google captures people searching the project name, the developer or the locality. Search campaign structure for projects is covered in real estate lead generation in India.
  2. Convince. The project page carries floor plans, a price band or "price on request" with a clear reason, RERA number, location map and construction updates. Property SEO helps these pages rank for locality and configuration searches over time.
  3. Capture. A short form or a click-to-WhatsApp button. Asking for budget and preferred configuration filters out casual enquiries.
  4. Qualify. A call or WhatsApp reply within minutes. Speed matters more here than almost anywhere; why leads don't answer calls explains the drop-off.
  5. Visit and close. The site visit is the real conversion. Everything before it should be judged on how many visits it produces.

A worked example of the numbers

Say a developer spends 6 lakh in a month across Meta and Google and gets 600 leads. Sales reaches 420, qualifies 150 as genuine buyers in the right budget, and books 45 site visits. Four bookings follow.

  • Cost per lead: 6 lakh ÷ 600 = 1,000.
  • Cost per site visit: 6 lakh ÷ 45, about 13,300.
  • Cost per booking: 6 lakh ÷ 4 = 1.5 lakh.

If Meta delivered 450 of the leads but only 15 of the visits, and Google delivered 150 leads and 30 visits, Google is producing visits at a far lower cost even though its cost per lead looks higher. Shifting budget on cost per lead alone would move money the wrong way. That comparison is only possible if the CRM records the source of every lead and sales updates each stage.

Splitting the budget by project stage

The right mix changes as a project moves. At pre-launch, Meta builds an interest list with an expression-of-interest form, and Google covers the developer name and locality. At launch, spend peaks across Meta, Google and portals while sales capacity is highest. During construction, search and remarketing carry most of the load, with construction-update videos for people who enquired earlier. Near possession, the remaining inventory is usually specific, so campaigns for a particular configuration or floor work better than broad project ads. NRI campaigns run alongside each stage, timed to the buyer's evening hours, with video calls offered instead of site visits.

What separates campaigns that sell units

  • Creative that shows the real thing: walkthrough video, the view from the actual floor, the commute shown on a map. Real estate ad creative has examples.
  • Locality pages, not just a project page, for buyers who search the area before the developer.
  • Lead quality fed back to the platforms, so Meta and Google learn which leads became visits.
  • A CRM the sales team actually uses; choosing a CRM covers simple options.

Questions people ask before hiring

What is real estate digital marketing?

Using portals, Google, Meta, a project website, SEO, WhatsApp and a CRM to find property buyers and move them to a site visit and a booking.

Which is better for real estate leads, portals or Meta ads?

Portals reach active buyers but share leads with competitors. Meta creates demand and reaches NRI buyers. Judge both on cost per site visit, not cost per lead.

What is a good cost per lead for real estate in India?

It depends on the project price and location. A better measure is cost per site visit and cost per booking, which show whether cheap leads are actually turning into buyers.

Do real estate websites need SEO?

Yes, especially locality and configuration pages. Buyers often search the area or a "2 BHK in" query before they know the developer.
