Twelve questions to ask an SEO agency before you hire one

SEO proposals all sound alike. These questions make them stop sounding alike.

Media Puppies · 18 October 2026

SEO is unusually hard to buy well, because most of the work is invisible and results take months. Proposals use the same vocabulary, promise the same outcomes and include the same audit screenshots. The way to tell them apart is to ask questions that only an agency doing real work can answer specifically. Here are the questions to ask an SEO agency, grouped by what they reveal.

Questions to ask an SEO agency about the work

1. What will you change on our site in the first month? Real SEO changes things: pages rewritten, technical fixes shipped, new pages built. "We will start with an audit" is fine as a first week, not as a first month.

2. Which keywords would you not chase, and why? A good agency has opinions about intent. It will tell you which high-volume terms bring the wrong visitors.

3. Who writes the content, and who checks it? AI drafting is normal. Publishing without a knowledgeable person reviewing it is a risk; does Google penalise AI content explains why.

4. What technical problems do you already see on our site? A prepared agency will have looked. Our technical SEO checklist lists the usual suspects.

Questions about links and risk

5. Where will links come from? Ask for examples from other clients. If the answer involves packages or networks, walk away; should you buy backlinks covers what happens next.

6. Have any of your clients had a manual action? An honest agency will answer, and explain what happened.

7. What happens to the work if we stop? Content and fixes on your site stay. Accounts and assets in the agency's name may not.

Questions about reporting and results

8. How will you show which pages produce enquiries? Rankings and traffic are inputs. Organic leads by landing page are the output.

9. What should we expect by month three and month six? Specific, not promised. Vague answers here usually mean vague work later.

10. Can we speak to a current client? Case studies are easy to write; a reference call is harder to fake. How to evaluate agency case studies has more on this.

Questions about fit

11. Would paid search be faster for us right now? An agency that sometimes says yes is putting your result ahead of its retainer.

12. Who will actually work on our account, and how many others do they handle? The senior person in the pitch is often not the person doing the work.

Answers that should make you walk away

Guaranteed rankings. Link packages. A fixed number of articles a month with no keyword plan. Reports that never mention enquiries. No changes to your site in month one. Questions people ask before hiring

What is the most important question to ask an SEO agency?

What they will change on your site in the first month. Real SEO work is visible on the site; an answer made up only of audits and reports is a warning sign.

Should an SEO agency guarantee rankings?

No. Nobody controls Google, and guarantees usually mean easy terms that bring no buyers. Ask for a plan tied to organic enquiries instead.

How do I check an SEO agency's link building?

Ask for examples of links built for other clients and where they came from. Packages, networks and guaranteed numbers of links are reasons to walk away.

What should an SEO report include?

Organic enquiries and sales by landing page, the changes made that month, and the plan for the next. Rankings and traffic belong underneath, as supporting evidence.
