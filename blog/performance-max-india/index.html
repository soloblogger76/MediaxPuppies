Performance Max for Indian businesses: how not to waste the budget

PMax will spend your budget efficiently on whatever you tell it to value. Getting that part right is the entire job.

Media Puppies · 29 August 2026 · Updated 11 October 2026

Performance Max is Google's most automated campaign type, and the least understood: one campaign, every inventory — Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail, Discover — with the machine deciding where your money goes.

It can work extremely well. It can also spend a month's budget on cheap Display impressions and report a glowing return built on conversions you would have got anyway. The difference is almost entirely in the setup.

Get the conversion definition right first

This is ninety percent of it. PMax optimises relentlessly towards the conversion you define. Define it loosely and it will find you loose conversions very efficiently.

  • For ecommerce: purchases with real values. Not add-to-cart, not page views.
  • For lead-gen: qualified leads, fed back from your CRM. Optimising to raw form fills in India reliably produces volume with no intent behind it.
  • Turn off secondary conversions as optimisation targets. Track them, do not chase them.

If you cannot send qualified-lead data back yet, fix that before running PMax. Everything downstream depends on it, and it is the same tracking work that makes every other channel more efficient.

The control you still have

PMax is often described as a black box. It is not quite:

  1. Brand exclusions. Without these, PMax will happily buy your own brand searches and take credit for customers who typed your name. This is the single biggest source of flattering, meaningless returns.
  2. Account-level negative keywords. Essential for cutting job seekers, free-seekers, and the wrong city.
  3. Asset quality. Your images, headlines and video are the real targeting lever. Weak assets mean the algorithm defaults to cheap placements.
  4. Audience signals. Not a hard target, but a genuine head start for the learning phase.
  5. Geographic targeting — and set it to presence, not presence-or-interest, unless you genuinely serve people researching from elsewhere.

Without brand exclusions, PMax will sell you your own customers and send you a nice report about it.

Give it a real asset group, not a placeholder

An asset group with three headlines and one stock image will underperform, and you will conclude PMax does not work. Fill every slot: the maximum headlines, long headlines, descriptions, images in all required ratios, and at least one video. If you do not supply video, Google generates one, and it is usually worse than anything you would have approved.

The reporting gap, and how to live with it

PMax reporting is thinner than standard campaigns. You can see more than the defaults suggest, though:

  • The search terms report at campaign level shows what queries triggered it.
  • Asset performance ratings tell you which creative is carrying the campaign.
  • Insights show audience and search themes driving conversions.

What you cannot cleanly see is the channel split. The practical workaround is the same as everywhere else in this business: run a holdout. Pause it for two weeks and watch whether blended cost per customer moves. If nothing changes, it was harvesting.

When not to use Performance Max

  • Very low conversion volume. Under roughly 30 conversions a month, it has nothing to learn from.
  • When you need to know what worked. For genuine testing, use campaign types that report properly.
  • Before your tracking is trustworthy. It will amplify bad signal faster than any other campaign type.

A sane launch sequence

Search first, on high-intent terms, with negative keywords maintained weekly. Once that is profitable and your conversion data is clean, add PMax with brand excluded and give it four weeks. Compare on blended cost per customer, not on the number PMax reports about itself.

Questions people ask

What is Performance Max?

A Google campaign type that runs across Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail and Discover from a single campaign, with Google deciding where the budget goes. You supply conversion goals, assets and audience signals; placement and bidding are automated.

Does Performance Max cannibalise branded search?

It can, and by default you will not see it in the reporting. Branded traffic absorbed into Performance Max makes the campaign look excellent while mostly buying demand you already had. Brand exclusions are the fix, and they have to be requested rather than toggled.

What control do I still have?

Conversion goals and their values, asset groups and audience signals, budget, brand exclusions, and the feed if you are running Shopping. That is a meaningful amount of control — most of the waste comes from leaving all of it at default.

When should I not use Performance Max?

When conversion tracking is not yet trustworthy, when volume is too low for the system to learn, or when you need to see exactly which query produced a sale. Automation amplifies whatever you point it at, including a wrong conversion definition.
