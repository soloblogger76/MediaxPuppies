Locksmith Google Ads cost in 2026: what a click costs in the US, UK, Canada and Australia

We took 200 locksmith keywords from Ahrefs across four countries and sorted them by what the searcher wants. The UK came out at twice the US price, and city searches beat the emergency terms most locksmiths bid on first.

Key findings

  • On Ahrefs estimates of 17 November 2026, the median locksmith buyer search cost $6 a click in the UK, $3 in the US, $3 in Canada and $2.25 in Australia. 100 clicks at the median: $600, $300, $300 and $225.
  • On the 11 locksmith keywords priced in all four countries, the UK was dearer than the US on 8. Australia and Canada were never dearer than the US on any of them, with medians at 72% of the US price.
  • City searches cost more than the plain word "locksmith". The US city median was $5 against $3 for "locksmith", and 15 of 18 US city searches were priced above it. The dearest single terms were "locksmith st kilda" at $16 and "locksmith croydon" at $12.
  • Emergency locksmith searches carried a small premium or none: 1.17x other locksmith searches in the US, 1.32x in Australia, 0.83x in the UK and 0.53x in Canada (2 keywords only).
  • On 6 US locksmith keywords in both tools, Ahrefs priced clicks at a median 52.48% of Semrush. Semrush put "emergency locksmith" at $6.62 and Ahrefs at $3.50.

Locksmith Google Ads are cheap by trade standards, and the UK is the exception. We took the top 50 locksmith keywords in Ahrefs for the US, the UK, Canada and Australia on 17 November 2026, 200 rows in all, and kept the 178 that are someone looking for a locksmith and carry a price. All prices are Ahrefs estimates in US dollars. They are modelled figures, and nobody outside Google sees the real auction.

The short answer. The median locksmith buyer search cost $6 a click in the UK, $3 in the US, $3 in Canada and $2.25 in Australia. A hundred clicks at those medians comes to $600, $300, $300 and $225. Inside each country, the dearest locksmith Google Ads clicks were city searches such as "locksmith denver" and "locksmith croydon". Emergency searches, the obvious first choice for a locksmith campaign, carried little or no premium.

If you run PPC for locksmiths, as an owner or as an agency, the sections below give the prices by country, by search type and by keyword, with the sample size beside each one.

The volume-weighted figure sits close to the median everywhere. In the US that is because two searches, "locksmith near me" and "locksmith", carry 78.49% of the buyer search volume in the list, and Ahrefs prices both at $3. City searches cost more than "locksmith near me"

This was the surprise. We expected the near-me and emergency searches to lead on price. In three of the four countries the dearest group was "locksmith" plus a city or suburb name.

In the US, the city median was $5 against $3 for the plain search "locksmith", and 15 of the 18 priced city searches cost more than it. The US top 10 by CPC was all cities, led by Denver and Phoenix at $9. In Canada 26 of 34 city searches were priced above "locksmith". In Australia the medians matched at $2.50, though the single dearest locksmith keyword in our data was there: "locksmith st kilda" at $16.

Emergency locksmith clicks carry little premium

Across all home-service trades in the parent study, emergency searches cost 3.33 times other searches in the US and 2.67 times in Australia. Locksmiths do not follow that pattern. The emergency median was 1.17 times other locksmith searches in the US ($3.50 against $3) and 1.32 times in Australia ($2.50 against $1.90). In the UK emergency searches came in below the rest, at 0.83 times, and in Canada at 0.53 times, though Canada's figure rests on only 2 keywords.

Our reading: for a plumber, "emergency" separates a burst pipe from a quote for a new bathroom. For a locksmith, most searches already are the emergency. In the US, Ahrefs shows 235,000 monthly searches for "locksmith near me" against 2,400 for "emergency locksmith". Someone locked out mostly types the plain search, so it carries the urgent demand and the price with it. Emergency terms still deserve their own ad group for the message ("there in 30 minutes", "open now"), and in our view they do not need a separate, bigger budget line for locksmiths the way they do for plumbers.

What a click is worth against a locksmith lead

We have no locksmith lead prices of our own. Two claims from our study of lead-cost claims on YouTube mention locksmiths. An agency presenter (John Wilson, Owned and Operated, July 2026) said Local Services Ads locksmith leads run at about $5 in the US. A software vendor (LeadTruffle, February 2026) put Angi locksmith leads at $25 to $50. Both are statements in videos, and neither shows an account. If the $5 figure is close for your area, Local Services Ads deserve a test beside search ads, since $5 is under two US locksmith clicks at the Ahrefs median.

What we would change in a locksmith PPC account

  • Build city ad groups first. On our data they are the dearest searches, so they need their own bids, ad text with the city name and a landing page that names the area. Our view is that one broad "locksmith" group lets city clicks eat the budget unseen.
  • Price the UK separately. At twice the US median, a UK locksmith campaign cannot share a plan with US or Canadian ones.
  • Split car and auto locksmith work. These searches were among the cheapest buyer groups outside the US. If you cut car keys, they are low-cost volume; if you do not, add them as negatives.
  • Watch the search terms report for brand names. 11 of our 200 rows were searches for a named locksmith business. Those clicks are someone else's customer. See our negative keywords audit.
  • Answer the phone. Locksmith searches are urgent whatever the keyword says, so calls need tracking and a fast answer. Our call tracking guide for home services covers the setup.

Questions about locksmith Google Ads and locksmith PPC

How much do locksmith Google Ads cost per click?

On Ahrefs estimates of 17 November 2026, the median locksmith buyer search cost $6 a click in the UK, $3 in the US, $3 in Canada and $2.25 in Australia. City searches cost more: the US city median was $5. Semrush puts US locksmith clicks higher, with a median of $5.80 across 15 keywords.

Are emergency locksmith keywords more expensive?

Only slightly, on our data. Emergency locksmith searches cost 1.17 times other locksmith searches in the US and 1.32 times in Australia, and less than other searches in the UK and Canada. Most locksmith searches are urgent already, so the plain terms carry the same demand.

Why are UK locksmith clicks dearer than US ones?

We can only describe it from the data. The UK median was $6 against $3 in the US, and the UK was dearer on 8 of 11 keywords found in both markets. London and South East towns such as Croydon, Brighton and Reading were the dearest UK searches at $11 to $12.

How much do 100 locksmith clicks cost?

At the Ahrefs median buyer CPC, 100 clicks come to about $600 in the UK, $300 in the US, $300 in Canada and $225 in Australia, in US dollars. Your own price depends on your bids, ads, quality and area, so check Keyword Planner and run a short test.

Is Google Ads or Local Services Ads better for locksmiths?

We have no measured locksmith lead prices. One agency presenter said US Local Services Ads locksmith leads run about $5, which is under two clicks at the Ahrefs median. Our view is to test both and compare cost per booked job.
