HVAC Google Ads cost by country: click prices in the US, Canada, Australia and the UK

We sorted 200 HVAC keywords from Ahrefs across four countries by what the searcher wants. In the US, emergency searches cost nine times the rest. Outside the US, the word "HVAC" turned out to belong to the trade more than to homeowners.

Key findings

  • On Ahrefs estimates of 17 November 2026, the median HVAC buyer search cost $2.50 a click in the US, $2 in Canada, $1.80 in Australia and $0.70 in the UK. 100 clicks at the median: $250, $200, $180 and $70.
  • US emergency HVAC searches had a median of $19 a click against $2 for the other 29 US buyer searches, a premium of 9.5x. "Emergency hvac service" was the dearest at $30. In the UK the 3 emergency searches cost $0.15, and Australia had none in its top 50.
  • The US top 50 HVAC list carried 30.42 times the monthly searches of the UK list and 68 times the Australian one. Half of the Australian list was not homeowner demand, and its dearest rows were HVAC software at $45 a click.
  • Canadian city searches were the dearest HVAC clicks outside the US: a median of $6 across 7 keywords, led by "hvac kingston" at $8.
  • On 7 US HVAC keywords in both tools, Ahrefs priced clicks at a median 11.31% of Semrush. Semrush put "hvac repair" at $30.91 and Ahrefs at $0.30.

HVAC Google Ads cost very different amounts depending on the country and on one word in the search. We took the top 50 HVAC keywords in Ahrefs for the US, the UK, Canada and Australia on 17 November 2026, 200 rows in all, and kept the 119 that are someone looking to buy HVAC work and carry a price. All prices are Ahrefs estimates in US dollars. They come from the tool's model, and nobody outside Google sees the real auction.

The short answer on HVAC Google Ads cost: the median buyer search was $2.50 a click in the US, $2 in Canada, $1.80 in Australia and $0.70 in the UK. A hundred clicks at those medians comes to $250, $200, $180 and $70. The median hides the US story, though. Six emergency searches had a median of $19, and they set the price of the urgent end of the market.

Our HVAC PPC guide covers how to set up the campaigns, and our HVAC LSA vs Google Ads study covers Local Services Ads and US prices by intent from Semrush. This page adds the other three countries and the keyword-by-keyword detail.

The order is what we expected: the US first and the UK last. The gap was bigger than we expected. Across all six home-service trades in the parent study, the UK median was $3 against $3.25 in the US. For HVAC the UK sits at 28% of the US price, and more than two in three UK HVAC keywords are priced under a dollar.

In the US, the volume-weighted figure ($3.56) sits above the median because the busiest searches are priced high: "hvac" itself at $3.50 with 328,000 monthly searches, and "hvac company" at $7 with 73,000. The US spread is wide, from $0.15 for "hvac near me" to $30 for "emergency hvac service".

Outside the US, "HVAC" is a trade word

Before reading the prices for the UK and Australia, look at the size of the lists. The US top 50 HVAC keywords add up to 2,023,000 monthly searches. The UK top 50 add up to 66,500, the Canadian to 135,400 and the Australian to 29,750. So the US list is 30.42 times the UK one, 14.94 times the Canadian one and 68 times the Australian one. The single search "hvac" gets 328,000 searches a month in the US, 28,000 in Canada, 22,000 in the UK and 6,300 in Australia.

What fills the lists is just as telling.

  • UK. 13 of the 50 rows contain "commercial": commercial hvac repair, maintenance, installation, systems, and six city versions of "commercial hvac repair" with no price yet. 15 rows are definitions, jobs, courses or tools. UK homeowners mostly say boiler, heating engineer or air conditioning, so "HVAC" in Britain reads as a building-services term.
  • Australia. Half the list (25 rows) is not someone buying HVAC work. The three dearest rows in the whole Australian pull were field service software for HVAC firms, at $35 to $45 a click. In Australia, the people paying most for the word "HVAC" are software vendors selling to contractors.
  • Canada. The list looks most like the US one, with company, contractor and service searches, plus 10 city rows. 13 rows were not buyer searches.

Our view: if you run HVAC PPC in the UK or Australia, the word "HVAC" is a small and mostly commercial slice of demand. A homeowner campaign there lives on boiler, heating, air conditioning and split system keywords. Those were outside this pull, so we have no prices for them, and we would not guess. The HVAC prices below are still useful for commercial work in those markets.

The US shows a smaller version of the same thing. In the Semrush US data we collected on 3 November 2026, searches with "hvac" in them were 29.51% of the 1,832,700 monthly searches across 28 HVAC keywords. Cooling words (ac, air conditioner) carried 650,500 searches at a median of $27.85, and heating words (furnace, heat pump) 641,400 at $18.20. "Ac repair" alone had 201,000 searches against 135,000 for "hvac repair". An HVAC account that bids only on "hvac" misses most of its own market, even in the US.

Emergency HVAC clicks cost 9.5 times the rest in the US

Six US buyer searches mention emergency. Their median was $19 a click, against $2 for the other 29 US buyer searches. That is a premium of 9.5x. Across all home-service trades in the parent study, the US emergency premium was 3.33x, so HVAC sits far above the average.

A hundred emergency clicks at the median cost $1,900, against $200 for a hundred of the other US searches. Yet the six emergency searches carry only 4.47% of US buyer search volume (77,000 of 1,721,300 monthly searches). Our reading: a broken AC in July or a dead furnace in January is high-value, same-day work, so a lot of contractors bid for a small pool of searches. The word order matters too. "Hvac emergency repair" was $6 with the most searches of the six, while "emergency hvac service" was $30.

None of this crossed the border. The three UK emergency searches had a median of $0.15, a fifth of the UK median. Canada had one emergency search in its top 50 ("emergency hvac repair", $0.90) and Australia had none. That matches the trade-word finding: outside the US, people with a broken heater mostly do not type "emergency hvac".

Commercial searches were the other dear US group, $11 across 2 keywords. "Commercial hvac near me" was $18, more than four times "commercial hvac" at $4. Two keywords make an indicator only.

In Canada, the city name sets the price

The US top 50 had no city searches at all, because national terms take every slot. In Canada, city searches were the dearest group, with a median of $6 across 7 keywords against $2 for Canada overall. In Australia the city median was $3.75 across 6.

Kingston at $8 above Toronto at $4 for the plain "hvac" plus city search surprised us. Smaller cities can have a handful of firms who all bid on the city name, while a big city's searchers spread across suburbs and near-me searches. Our view for Canadian HVAC accounts: give each city its own ad group, so a dear city does not drain the budget meant for the rest of the service area.

Near me did not behave as a premium. In the US the 14 near-me searches had a median of $1.80 against $3 for the rest. Of 13 US pairs where we have a keyword with and without "near me", the near-me version was dearer in 5. "Hvac repair near me" was $5 against $0.30 for "hvac repair", and "hvac maintenance near me" $1.60 against $9 for "hvac maintenance". Those swings are too large and too random to plan around.

The median ratio was 11.31%. Across all 52 keywords both tools priced in the parent study, it was 36.52%. On 3 of the 7, Ahrefs was under a tenth of Semrush, and those were three of the busiest US HVAC searches: "hvac repair", "hvac near me" and "hvac installation". On the emergency and company searches the two tools were within about three times of each other.

Our view: for core US HVAC repair and near-me searches, the Ahrefs figures look like gaps in its model. A $0.30 click for a 128,000-search repair term does not fit a market where Semrush prices the same search at $30.91 and the emergency version at $33.30. For US HVAC budgets we would plan from the Semrush range, about $18 to $31 for the core searches, use Ahrefs for the shape of the market between countries, and let a two-week live test with Keyword Planner settle the real figure.

What one HVAC lead buys in clicks

We have no HVAC lead prices of our own. The lead claims we collected from YouTube are covered in our HVAC LSA study. One for scale: Sam Preston of Service Scalers, on the John Wilson channel in October 2026, said US HVAC Local Services Ads leads had risen from $51 to $60. That is a presenter's claim. Simple division puts a $60 lead at 24 clicks at the Ahrefs US median, 3.06 clicks at the Semrush US median and 3.16 emergency clicks at the Ahrefs emergency median. Which of those is closest to your market decides whether search ads or LSA look cheaper per lead, and only your own call log can tell you.

What we would change in an HVAC Google Ads account

  1. Bid on what homeowners say. In the US that means AC, furnace and heat pump keywords next to "hvac", which carried 29.51% of the Semrush search volume. In the UK and Australia it means boiler, heating and air conditioning keywords, and "hvac" for commercial work only.
  2. Fence off emergency terms in the US. At a median of $19, emergency clicks need their own campaign, ad schedule and budget cap, and a phone line that is answered at night. Our call tracking guide covers the setup.
  3. Split Canadian cities. At $6 against $2, city searches deserve their own ad groups and landing pages.
  4. Clean the search terms report. 30% of the US list and half the Australian one were not buyer searches: jobs, courses, definitions, suppliers, software and news. Those clicks will never book a job. Our negative keywords audit lists the usual offenders.
  5. Plan from a range. One keyword tool is not enough for HVAC. Take the Ahrefs and Semrush figures as low and high ends, then test.

Questions about HVAC Google Ads and HVAC PPC cost

How much do HVAC Google Ads cost per click?

On Ahrefs estimates of 17 November 2026, the median HVAC buyer search cost $2.50 a click in the US, $2 in Canada, $1.80 in Australia and $0.70 in the UK. Semrush prices US HVAC much higher: a median of $19.59 across 28 keywords on 3 November 2026. Treat the two tools as a low and a high end.

Why are emergency HVAC keywords so expensive in the US?

On our data, the six US emergency searches had a median of $19 a click, 9.5 times the other US HVAC searches, yet they carried only 4.47% of the search volume. Many contractors bid for a small pool of urgent, high-value jobs. Outside the US, emergency HVAC searches were rare and cheap.

Is HVAC PPC cheaper in the UK and Australia?

For keywords containing "hvac", yes: the UK median was 28% of the US and Australia 72%. But the word is used far less there. The US list had 30.42 times the UK searches and 68 times the Australian ones, and much of the non-US demand sits under boiler, heating and air conditioning keywords we did not price.

How much do 100 HVAC clicks cost?

At the Ahrefs median buyer CPC, 100 clicks come to about $250 in the US, $200 in Canada, $180 in Australia and $70 in the UK, in US dollars. 100 US emergency clicks at the median come to $1,900. Your own price depends on your bids, ads, quality and area.

Which keyword tool should I trust for HVAC CPC?

Neither alone. On 7 US HVAC keywords, Ahrefs was a median 11.31% of Semrush, and on "hvac repair" it was under 1%. Our view is to use both as a range, check Google Keyword Planner for your own area and run a short test before setting a budget.
