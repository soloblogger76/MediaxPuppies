Roofing Google Ads cost in 2026: what a roofer click costs in the US, UK, Canada and Australia

We pulled the top 50 "roofer" searches in four countries from Ahrefs and priced the ones where someone wants a roofer. The US came out cheapest by a wide margin, and the reason says more about the word than the market.

Key findings

  • On Ahrefs estimates of 17 November 2026, the median roofer buyer search cost $4.00 a click in Australia, $3.50 in the UK, $3.50 in Canada and $1.45 in the US. 100 clicks at those medians: $400, $350, $350 and $145.
  • The US figure is thin. Only 8 of 41 US buyer keywords for "roofer" had any price, and 32 of the unpriced rows were neighbourhood or business-park names. In the UK, 43 of 46 had a price.
  • Semrush prices US roofing job searches far higher: a median of $20.27 across 25 keywords such as "roof repair" and "roofing company", 13.98 times the Ahrefs US roofer median. Semrush had "roofers near me" at $26.83, while Ahrefs had "roofer near me" at $1.50.
  • "Emergency roofer" was the dearest roofer keyword in any country, $20 in the US, or 14.29 times the word "roofer". In the UK emergency searches cost 0.88 times "roofer", and Australia had none in its top 50.
  • Outside the US, roofer clicks sat a little above each country's home-services median, at 114.29% to 116.67% of it. In the US they sat at 44.62%.

Roofing Google Ads cost depends heavily on the words in the keyword list, and the word "roofer" shows it better than any other. We took the top 50 Ahrefs keywords for "roofer" in the US, the UK, Canada and Australia on 17 November 2026, 200 rows in all, removed the ones where nobody wants a roof fixed, and priced the 92 buyer searches that had a figure. Every price here is an Ahrefs or Semrush estimate in US dollars, since nobody outside Google sees the real auction.

The short answer on roofing Google Ads cost per click: the median "roofer" buyer search was $4.00 in Australia, $3.50 in the UK, $3.50 in Canada and $1.45 in the US. We expected the US to top that list, as it does for most trades. It came last. Our reading is that Americans looking for a roof mostly search "roof repair", "roofing company" or "roofers", which Semrush prices at around $20 a click. The singular "roofer" barely has a market there.

If you run roofer PPC for your own firm or for clients, the sections below give the prices by country, the gaps in the data, the US job searches that carry the money and what a click is worth next to a lead. For campaign setup and ad copy, see our roofing ads guide and our study of 100 roof replacement ads; this page sticks to click prices.

Three of the four markets look ordinary. Australia, the UK and Canada sit just above the median for all home-service searches in the same Ahrefs pull, which was $3.50, $3.00 and $3.00. A roofer click there costs about what a plumber or electrician click costs, give or take a few cents.

The US breaks the pattern, and its middle half running from $0.60 to $6.12 tells you why the median is shaky. Eight priced keywords is a small base. The two head terms, "roofer near me" at 40,000 searches a month and "roofer" at 32,000, are priced at $1.50 and $1.40, and they decide the median almost alone.

Most US roofer searches have no price at all

Coverage was the first thing that looked wrong. In the UK, 43 of the 46 buyer searches carried a CPC. In the US, 8 of 41 did. The other 33 were mostly long place names: neighbourhoods, business parks and small towns, many with 1,000 or more monthly searches in Ahrefs and no price. 32 of them were city or suburb searches, adding up to 34,900 searches a month.

A missing CPC in Ahrefs means the tool saw too little advertiser activity to estimate one. We read it as a sign that roofers in those places are bidding on other words. Australia and Canada sit in between, with about half of their roofer searches priced, again with the gaps in smaller suburbs and towns.

Where the US roofing money sits: job searches

To see what US roofing searches cost when the searcher names the job, we went back to the 25 US roofing keywords we priced in Semrush on 3 November 2026. None is identical to an Ahrefs "roofer" row, so this is a separate list from a separate tool.

The Semrush median across all 25 was $20.27, which is 13.98 times the Ahrefs US roofer median. Near-me and emergency searches led. "Hail damage roof repair" was the dearest single keyword at $35.99, and "emergency roof repair" was $32.45. Cost research was the cheap end, from $5.22 for "metal roof cost" to $9.15 for "roof repair cost".

The nearest thing to a like-for-like check is two pairs that differ by one letter. Ahrefs priced "roofer near me" at $1.50 and Semrush priced "roofers near me" at $26.83, 17.89 times as much. "Roofer" at $1.40 against "roofers" at $17.57 is 12.55 times. Across the 52 identical keywords in the parent study, Ahrefs came in at a median 36.52% of Semrush, so the tools disagree on every trade. A gap this wide, though, is more than tool noise. Our view is that the plural is what US homeowners type and the singular is closer to a job title, so advertisers bid on one and ignore the other.

Emergency roofer searches: a US spike, flat elsewhere

"Emergency roofer" at $20 was the dearest buyer keyword in the whole dataset, 14.29 times the US price of "roofer". It is a single keyword, so we treat it as a pointer, and Semrush points the same way: its four US emergency and storm searches had a median of $27.58.

The other countries did not follow. UK emergency searches had a median of $3.50, which is 0.88 times "roofer". Canada's two were $4, or 1.33 times. Australia had no emergency search in its top 50. In the parent study, emergency searches across all home-service trades cost 3.33 times other searches in the US and 1.8 times in the UK, so roofing fits the US pattern and sits below it in the UK.

Our view on why: in the US, storm and hail seasons send a burst of urgent searches into a market where insurance often pays for the roof, and that combination draws heavy bidding. We cannot test that from keyword data, and we flag it as a reading.

City searches outside the US

In the UK, city searches made up 35 of the 43 priced buyer keywords, with a median of $3.50, the same as the country. 13 of them were priced above "roofer". The dearest were in the South East: "roofer sutton" at $10 and "roofer fareham" and "roofer guildford" at $8.

What a click is worth next to a roofing lead

We have no roofing lead prices of our own. Our study of lead-cost claims on YouTube logged several for roofing, and these are claims made by presenters. In an October 2026 video, an agency presenter (John Wilson, Owned and Operated) cited a figure of $107 per roofing lead from Local Services Ads, up from $74 a year earlier. A software vendor (LeadTruffle, February 2026) put Angi roofing leads at $50 to $120 or more, sold to several contractors at once.

Set against click prices, $107 buys about 4 clicks at the Semrush price of "roof repair near me". At the Ahrefs price of "roofer near me" it would be about 71 clicks, which shows how much the choice of keyword list changes the sum. Our view: for US roofing, plan from the Semrush job-search range, since that is where the competing roofers bid.

What we would change in a roofer PPC account

  • Build the US account on job words. "Roof repair", "roof replacement", "roofing company" and "roofers near me" are where the searches and the bids sit. Keep "roofer" as a cheap extra, and expect little volume from it.
  • Do not set a US budget from a "roofer" CPC. A $1.45 median would understate the real cost of a roofing campaign many times over. Use the job-search range and let a short live test settle it.
  • Treat the UK, Canada and Australia as normal-priced trades. At $3.50 to $4.00 a click, "roofer" and "roofer + town" are usable core keywords there. In the UK, give South East towns their own bids.
  • Keep emergency and storm terms in their own campaign in the US. They cost the most in both tools. In our view they deserve a separate budget, so a hail week cannot drain the money meant for replacement leads.
  • Add the sellers as negatives. "Roofer seo", "roofer insurance", "roofer jobs" and "roofer certification" all appeared in the top 50. Our negative keywords audit covers the routine.

On 17 November 2026 we opened the Ahrefs "Matching terms" report for "roofer" in the US, UK, Australian and Canadian databases and kept the top 50 keywords by monthly search volume. Ahrefs shows CPC in US dollars for every country. Half of the 200 rows had no CPC; they count toward coverage and stay out of every median.

The parent study flags non-buyer searches with a written rule (jobs, salary, insurance, definitions). It caught 9 roofer rows. We read the rest by eye and set 13 more to non-buyer for this page: 4 searches for a named business, 3 place names from another country (two New South Wales towns and a Scottish town in the US list) and 6 searches for things sold to roofers, such as "roofer seo" and "roofer certification". The list and the reason for each are in compile.py and data.csv.

Each buyer keyword sits in one group, first match wins: emergency, repair, commercial, residential, material or roof type, best/licensed/insured, near me, generic ("roofer", "local roofer", "roofer company", "roofer contractors") and city or suburb.

Questions about roofing Google Ads and roofer PPC

How much do roofing Google Ads cost per click?

It depends on the keywords. On Ahrefs estimates of 17 November 2026, the median roofer buyer search cost $4.00 in Australia, $3.50 in the UK, $3.50 in Canada and $1.45 in the US. US roofing job searches such as "roof repair" and "roofing company" are far dearer: Semrush priced 25 of them at a median of $20.27.

Why is "roofer" so cheap in the US?

Ahrefs sees little bidding on it. Only 8 of 41 US roofer buyer searches had any Ahrefs price, and "roofer near me" was $1.50 while Semrush put "roofers near me" at $26.83. Our view is that US homeowners search the plural or the job, and roofers bid there.

Are emergency roofing keywords more expensive?

In the US, yes. "Emergency roofer" was $20 in Ahrefs, 14.29 times "roofer", and Semrush priced US emergency and storm roofing searches at a median of $27.58. In the UK emergency searches cost slightly less than "roofer", and Canada's cost slightly more.

How much do 100 roofer clicks cost?

At the Ahrefs median buyer CPC, about $400 in Australia, $350 in the UK, $350 in Canada and $145 in the US, in US dollars. For the US we would plan from the Semrush job-search range instead, since that is where roofers compete.

Should roofers use Local Services Ads or search ads?

We have no measured roofing lead prices. One agency presenter cited $107 per US roofing lead from Local Services Ads, which is about 4 clicks at the Semrush price of "roof repair near me". Our view is to run both and compare cost per signed job.
