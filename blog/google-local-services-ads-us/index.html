Local Services Ads: the US listing above your search ads

You can be first on paid search and still be fourth on the page. Local Services Ads sit above everything, charge per lead rather than per click, and most advertisers never work them properly.

Local Services Ads (LSA) sit above the normal search ads on many US home service searches, with the Google Verified badge, a star rating and a call button, and they are what a lot of people tap first. You pay per lead rather than per click. In 2026 the product changed more than in any year since launch: it is moving into Google Ads, missed calls are now charged, and the Google Guaranteed badge is gone.

How Local Services Ads differ from search ads

  • You pay per lead. Google charges for valid phone calls, messages and booking requests rather than clicks.
  • There is no landing page. The listing is the destination. Your profile, reviews and how fast you answer do the convincing.
  • Bids matter less than in search. Proximity, review score and volume, responsiveness, hours and complaints all feed ranking alongside your budget and bid.
  • There are no keywords. You pick service categories, job types and the areas you serve.
  • Entry is gated. Licence, insurance and background checks come before the first lead.

What LSA leads cost in 2026

Google publishes no price list. The cost per lead depends on trade, city and competition, and message leads usually cost less than phone leads. In our study of 25 YouTube videos on Angi, Thumbtack and LSA lead costs, Sam Preston of the agency Service Scalers read out late 2026 cost per lead by trade against a year earlier. He did not say whether the figures were LSA only.

Verification is still the slow part

Verification takes weeks, and the licence holder has to complete it; an agency cannot do it for you. Licences, insurance certificates and background check consent all come from you. We ask for it in week one, because discovering it in month three costs a quarter of placement.

Advertisers who were already verified moved to the Google Verified badge automatically, and the badge carries over into Google Ads. Google says that after the move, insurance and licence re-verification are no longer required.

Bad leads are credited automatically

LSA used to rely on advertisers disputing bad leads by hand. Google now reviews leads itself: leads it judges invalid or low quality are not charged, and charged leads can be credited automatically, usually within 30 days, according to its page on credits. It no longer credits a lead for a job type or area you do not serve, so keep your job types and service areas tight. That setting now does the work a dispute used to.

Rate each lead in the account, because Google uses the feedback to send better-matched leads. Agencies on YouTube disagree about whether a manual route still exists: one says leads have not been disputable for a year or two, another says about 30% of its disputes are now accepted.

Reviews are the ranking lever

Reviews carry heavy weight, both the score and how recently they arrived. A steady trickle beats a burst, and a business with thirty reviews from this year will generally outrank one with two hundred from four years ago.

The practical version: ask at the point of completion, while the technician is still there and the customer is pleased, rather than three days later by email.

Answer the phone

Responsiveness feeds ranking and conversion at the same time, and since October 2026 a missed call can also cost you a lead charge. For most home service businesses the cheapest improvement available is making sure somebody picks up, including at the hours when people have emergencies. Agencies in our study gave the same rules of thumb: answer at least 75% of calls live and reply to messages within 15 minutes.

Run it alongside search

LSA captures people who want to call someone now. Search captures research, comparison and every query LSA does not serve. Planned work, such as renovations and system replacements, still belongs in search with a proper landing page; our study of 169 home service keyword prices shows what those clicks cost.

Budget them separately and judge them separately. A blended cost per lead across both hides the fact that they are buying two different kinds of customer.

Before you start

Work out what a job is actually worth to you, because LSA charges per lead and leads are not jobs. If you are not already tracking which calls become booked work, fix that first. Questions people ask

How do Local Services Ads differ from search ads?

You pay per lead rather than per click, the listing carries the Google Verified badge and your review rating, and you cannot write the ad or choose keywords. Ranking comes from proximity, reviews, responsiveness, hours and complaints alongside your budget and bid.

How much do Local Services Ads cost per lead?

Google publishes no price list. One agency CEO read out late 2026 figures of $107 a lead for roofing, $66 for plumbing, $60 for HVAC, $43 for electrical and $34 for appliance repair, without saying whether they were LSA only. Typical agency ranges ran from $40 to $70, with some accounts at $100 to $300 or more.

What changed with Local Services Ads in 2026?

From August 2026 Google began moving LSA campaigns into Google Ads in phases, as Performance Max campaigns with pay-per-lead goals. Weekly budgets become daily and maximum cost-per-lead bids go. Since 1 October 2026, missed calls over 20 seconds in business hours are charged.

Can I dispute a bad lead?

Google now reviews leads itself. Leads it judges invalid or low quality are not charged, and charged leads can be credited automatically, usually within 30 days. It no longer credits leads for job types or areas you do not serve, so keep those settings tight and rate each lead.

What does verification involve?

Business registration, licence and insurance documents, and background checks on the business and often its owners and field staff. It is the slowest part of the setup, commonly taking weeks, so start it before you plan the budget.

Should I run Local Services Ads instead of search ads?

Run them alongside search. The two occupy different positions on the same result, and search ads still cover the terms and services the Local Services categories do not. Dropping search usually costs more volume than the saving is worth.
