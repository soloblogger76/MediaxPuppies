10 things that lower cost per lead this week

Not strategy. Ten specific changes, ordered by how fast they pay, that most accounts have not made yet.

Media Puppies · 29 July 2026 · Updated 11 October 2026

Most advice on how to lower cost per lead takes a quarter to apply. These take an afternoon, and they are ordered roughly by how quickly they show up in the numbers.

1. Change the offer, not the targeting

Contact us for a quote asks for effort and promises nothing. Get a room-by-room quote in 24 hours, no site visit promises something specific. Same audience, same budget, different cost per lead. This is almost always the biggest single lever and the least used.

2. Cut two form fields

Every field costs completions. Ask for what you need to make the first call — usually name, phone and one qualifying question. The rest can be asked while you have them on the line.

3. Read your search terms report

For Google accounts, an hour here regularly finds 20–30% of spend going to job seekers, free-seekers and the wrong city. The full audit process is here, and it is the highest-return boring work available.

4. Send a qualified-lead signal back to the platform

If you optimise for form fills, you get people who fill forms. Send back which leads were actually qualified and the algorithm starts hunting for people who look like your good leads instead. Cost per lead may rise; cost per customer falls, which is the number that matters.

5. Add an instant WhatsApp reply

Does not change cost per lead at all — it changes what a lead is worth, which has the same effect on your economics. A lead answered in minutes converts dramatically better than one answered in hours.

6. Check frequency on your cold audiences

Above roughly 2.5 to 3, you are paying to show the same ad to people who already decided. Either refresh the creative or widen the audience. Rising frequency with rising cost is fatigue, not a bad concept.

7. Exclude your existing customers

Upload your customer list as an exclusion on prospecting campaigns. Plenty of accounts are quietly paying to acquire people who already bought.

8. Kill the bottom third

Most accounts have ads that have spent real money with nothing to show, kept alive because nobody decided. Pause anything that has spent three times your target CPA with no conversions. Do it today, not after one more weekend.

9. Make the landing page fast on a real phone

Test on a mid-range Android on 4G. Every second past three costs you a share of arrivals before they see anything. Compressing the hero image is frequently a ten-minute fix with a measurable return.

10. Match the ad to the page

If the ad says one thing and the page sells another, you pay for the click and lose the person. Go through your top five ads and check each one lands somewhere that repeats its promise. This takes twenty minutes and it is astonishing how often it is broken.

Nine of these ten cost nothing except attention. That is usually what is actually scarce.

What will not lower cost per lead, despite what you have read

Do not start by raising the budget. More spend on a leaky funnel buys more leaks. And do not rebuild the campaign structure before checking the four things above it — structure is the lever people reach for because it feels like work, and it is rarely the problem.

Questions people ask

What is the fastest way to lower cost per lead?

Change the offer, not the targeting. What you are asking someone to do moves response rate far more than which audience you ask. A free audit outperforms "get in touch" on the same traffic.

Does narrowing the audience lower cost per lead?

Usually the opposite. Narrow audiences cost more per impression and starve the campaign of the conversions it needs to optimise against. Broader targeting with sharper creative is more reliable.

How much do form fields matter?

A lot, in both directions. Removing two fields raises completion; adding one qualifying field raises quality and lowers volume. Which you want depends on whether your sales team is short of leads or short of time.

How quickly should these changes show up?

Offer and landing page changes can move the number within a week. Structural changes need the learning phase to settle, so give those a fortnight before reading anything into them.
