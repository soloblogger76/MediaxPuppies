What's actually included.

Six workstreams. Not all six run every month — the audit decides the order.

01

Technical SEO

The plumbing. If Google can't crawl it, index it, or load it fast enough on a 4G phone, nothing else you do matters.

  • Crawl and index audit — what Google can actually see
  • Core Web Vitals and real mobile speed
  • Site structure, internal links, redirects
  • Schema markup so your listing shows more than a blue link
  • Search Console errors cleared, not just reported
02

Keyword research from money, not guesses

Most agencies open a keyword tool and sort by volume. If you run ads, your own account already knows which searches produce customers.

  • Converting search terms pulled from your ad account
  • Buying-intent keywords separated from research keywords
  • Competitor gap analysis — what they rank for and you don't
  • A ranked plan led by revenue, not traffic
03

Content that answers buying questions

Not 2,000 words on "the importance of" anything. Pages that answer what a person types right before they spend money.

  • Service and location pages built to rank and to convert
  • Comparison, pricing and "how much does X cost" pages
  • Articles written around angles already tested in ads
  • Existing pages rewritten before new ones get written
04

Local SEO & Google Business Profile

If you have a catchment area — a clinic, a showroom, a studio — the map pack is often worth more than page one.

  • Google Business Profile fully built and maintained
  • Location pages for the areas you actually serve
  • A review flow that doesn't annoy your customers
  • Name, address and phone consistent across directories
05

Authority, earned not bought

We don't buy link packages. Cheap links are the fastest route to a manual penalty, and penalties take longer to undo than rankings take to build.

  • Digital PR and genuine editorial placements
  • Industry and association listings that carry weight in India
  • Internal linking — free, and badly underused
  • Toxic backlink audit, and disavow where it's needed
06

  • Organic leads and revenue, not just sessions

What the first six months look like.

Fix and plan

Full technical audit and the fixes that come out of it. Search Console and GA4 set up properly. Keyword plan built from your ad data. Errors drop — rankings don't move yet, and shouldn't.

Months 2–3

Publish and build

First content batch goes live. Weak existing pages rewritten. Google Business Profile built out. Early movement on lower-competition terms.

Months 4–6

Compound

Steady publishing plus authority work. Money keywords reach page two, then page one. Organic leads become a number you can count.

Month 6+

Cut your ad bill

You now rank for terms you were paying for. We drop paid bids on those and move the budget to terms you don't own yet. Blended cost per customer falls.

Straight answers.

How long before I see results?

Three to six months for meaningful movement, longer in competitive categories. Anyone promising page one in 30 days is either buying links or lying. If you need customers this month, run ads — and start SEO alongside so next year is cheaper.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No, and nobody honestly can — Google doesn't sell placements. What we commit to is the work: what gets fixed, what gets published, and a monthly report showing both. If the numbers aren't moving by month four, we raise it before you have to.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Month to month, like everything else we do. If it stops working, you stop paying.

Do you buy backlinks?

No. Cheap link packages are the fastest route to a manual penalty, and a penalty takes far longer to undo than rankings take to build. We earn placements instead.

Can you do SEO if I don't run ads?

Yes. Running ads makes the keyword research sharper, because we can see which searches actually convert — but it isn't required.

Who writes the content?

Our team, briefed on your business and reviewed by someone who has run ads in your category. You approve everything before it publishes.
