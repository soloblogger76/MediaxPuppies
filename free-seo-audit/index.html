The questions everyone asks

How long does SEO actually take?+

Three to six months before it produces meaningful enquiries in most Indian categories, longer in competitive ones like real estate and legal. Anyone promising faster is describing paid ads, or lying. The audit will give you a specific estimate for your site rather than a range.

Do you guarantee rankings?+

No. Nobody controls Google’s algorithm, and every guarantee we have seen is written around keywords with no search volume — which is how the agency wins the argument and you get nothing.

What does the free audit actually include?+

A written document: what is technically blocking the site, the keyword and content gap against competitors, the three to five fixes we would do first, and an honest view on whether SEO is your best next channel. It is written by a person and takes us a couple of hours, which is why we ask qualifying questions before doing it.

Is the audit really free, with no catch?+

Yes. You keep it whether you hire us or not. We ask for a budget range up front because writing these properly takes time, and we would rather spend it on businesses we could actually help.

Do you buy backlinks?+

No. Paid link packages are the fastest way to a problem that takes months to undo. We earn links with things worth linking to — tools, data and genuinely useful pages.

What if we already have an agency?+

That is common, and the audit is often how people find out what they are paying for. We will tell you if their work looks sound — there is no advantage to us in pretending otherwise.
