Straight answers.

What is a CAC ceiling?

The most you can pay to acquire a customer and still make money. It comes from your own gross profit per order, how many times a customer buys, and what share of that profit you're willing to spend on acquisition. It is not an industry benchmark — it's specific to your business.

Why not just use an industry benchmark?

Because a Rs 300 CAC is excellent for a Rs 2,000 order and ruinous for a Rs 400 one. Published benchmarks also usually come from brands with different margins, repeat rates and discounting habits, and most quietly leave returns out.

What share of lifetime profit should I spend?

A third is a common, defensible answer for a business funding itself from cash flow. Higher is a growth decision that needs funding behind it and a plan, not an accident.

Should I include returns in the margin?

Yes. Use margin after cost of goods, shipping both ways, payment gateway, packaging and your realistic return rate. COD-heavy categories should be especially strict here, because return-to-origin eats the number.

What if my actual CAC is above the ceiling?

You have three levers: raise average order value, improve repeat purchase rate, or lower acquisition cost. The first two raise the ceiling, which is usually easier than lowering the cost.
