Three ways to work with us

Three models, priced differently.

What you pay depends on which of these you need. Most clients are on the first; agencies are usually on the third.

Managed retainer

We run the accounts, you approve the direction. A flat monthly fee set by how much spend is under management and how many channels are running — because those two things determine the work, and nothing else does. Everything in the scope below is included. Month to month.

Dedicated media buyer

A named senior buyer working inside your team, your Slack and your standup. A flat monthly fee for the person, independent of how much they spend. This suits teams that already have strategy and creative and need execution. Details on the dedicated buyer page.

White label, per account

For agencies. A fixed monthly fee per client account, invoiced to you, with your brand on everything that reaches your client. You set your own price to them and keep the spread. No minimum number of accounts.

Scope

What the fee covers, and what it does not.

The most common reason agency relationships sour is a scope nobody wrote down. Here is ours, before you ask for it.

Included in every retainer

Account audit and rebuild, campaign structure and build, daily management and monitoring, bidding and budget pacing, audience and keyword work, product feed setup and optimisation, pixel and Conversions API implementation, conversion tracking and CRM conversion imports, ad copywriting, creative direction and a testing roadmap, disapproval handling and appeals, a weekly written report with the decisions in it, and a monthly call.

Not included by default

Video and photo production, design beyond simple static adaptation, landing page build and development, influencer and creator fees, email and SMS marketing, organic social management, and any third-party software licences such as call tracking or a consent platform. Any of these can be scoped, and we would rather quote them separately than bury them and under-deliver.

Never charged

No setup fee. No onboarding fee. No percentage of ad spend. No charge for an extra report or an extra call. No exit fee, and no charge to hand your accounts back — you already own them.

How we quote

We quote after the audit, and here is why.

Publishing a price list would be easier for both of us, and it would be wrong often enough to matter.

The audit tells us the actual work

Two businesses at identical spend can need entirely different amounts of work. One has clean tracking, a working feed and a creative team; the other needs the tracking rebuilt before anything can be optimised. Quoting before seeing that means either padding the price or discovering the gap later and resenting it.

What moves the number

Up: more channels, several markets or languages, a feed with thousands of SKUs, a CRM integration for offline conversions, or a regulated category needing compliance review on every asset. Down: a single channel, a single market, clean existing tracking, and a client team that can produce its own creative.

What we will not do to win the work

We will not quote a low first-quarter fee and raise it once you are dependent. We will not promise a ROAS or a cost per lead to close a deal — anyone doing that before seeing your margins is guessing and building an excuse in advance. And if the audit says paid media cannot work for you yet, we will tell you and not send a quote at all.

Terms

Month to month, in your currency.

The commercial terms are deliberately boring and deliberately easy to leave.

No lock-in

Thirty days’ notice, any time, no penalty. Long contracts exist to protect agencies from the consequences of bad work. If we are worth keeping you will keep us, and if we are not, a twelve-month term only delays your decision and wastes your budget in the meantime.

Your currency, fixed

Invoiced in dollars, pounds, dirhams, Australian dollars or rupees. The figure in your contract is in your currency and does not move with the exchange rate. The rupee prices shown across this site are converted for guidance using your location; the quote you receive is the real number.

You own everything from day one

Ad accounts, pixels and datasets, feeds, audiences, conversion tracking, creative files and reporting templates are all in your name. We hold partner access and you can revoke it in two clicks, without asking us. There is no offboarding fee because there is nothing to hand over.

Straight answers about money.

Why will you not publish a price?

Because the honest number depends on work we cannot see until we look at the account. A published figure would be too high for simple accounts and too low for complicated ones, and we would end up padding or resenting. The audit is free and the quote follows it.

Why not charge a percentage of ad spend?

Because it pays us to spend more of your money. When the right advice is to cut budget on a channel that has stopped working, a percentage model makes that advice expensive for us to give. A flat fee removes the conflict entirely.

Is there a setup or onboarding fee?

No. The first month includes the audit, the tracking work and the rebuild, at the same monthly fee as every other month.

What is the minimum commitment?

One month. Thirty days' notice after that, at any point, with no penalty and no exit fee.

Do you take equity, commission or performance-only deals?

No. Performance-only arrangements sound attractive and they distort behaviour — they push an agency toward short-term tactics and toward claiming credit for demand you already had. We charge a fee for work and report honestly on results.

What currency will we be invoiced in?

Yours. USD, GBP, AED, AUD, CAD, EUR or INR. The contract figure is fixed in that currency.

What happens to our accounts if we leave?

Nothing. They are already in your name. You remove our access and everything — history, audiences, creative, tracking — stays exactly where it is.

Free audit, then a number.

In that order. You will know what is wrong with the account before you know what we cost.
