Questions students actually ask.

Is there any fee to apply or join?

No. Never. We pay you, not the other way round. Any message asking you for a registration fee, deposit or training charge in our name is a fraud — please report it to us.

Can I do this along with college?

Yes — that's who it's designed for. 3–4 hours a day, and you choose which hours. Deadlines matter; timings don't. Tell us in the call if you have exams coming up.

I have zero experience. Should I still apply?

Yes. The writing task decides it, not your CV. We've hired people whose only writing was Instagram captions.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, on completion — plus a letter of recommendation if your work earns it. You also keep links to everything you published.

Is it really work from home?

Fully remote, anywhere in India. No office visits. Everything runs over WhatsApp, email and one weekly video call.

Can it turn into a full-time job?

Yes. That is genuinely the main way we hire — several of our team joined as interns first. We can't promise it to everyone on day one, but if your work is good, you get the offer before the two months end.
