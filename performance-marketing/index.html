What a performance marketing agency does.

A performance marketing agency buys ads on platforms like Google and Meta and is judged on a measurable result: a lead, a sale, a booked call. It runs the campaigns, the creative tests and the tracking, and it reports what each customer cost. If the number is not moving, the plan changes.

That is the difference from a brand campaign, which is judged on reach and recall. Both have a place. Brand spend is covered in performance marketing vs brand marketing. If you want to know what the work involves before you talk to anyone, read what performance marketing costs.

Judged on

Cost per qualified lead, cost per customer and return on ad spend. Not impressions or follower counts.

Changes

Every week. New creative, new audiences, budget moved to what is working and away from what is not.

Needs from you

Access to the ad accounts, honest margin numbers, and someone who calls leads back quickly.

How to choose a performance marketing agency.

Whoever you hire, us included, these five checks separate an agency that manages accounts from one that grows them.

  • The ad accounts stay in your name. If they will not agree to that in writing, walk away. More in who owns your ad account.
  • No guaranteed ROAS before they have seen your margins. A promise made blind is a sales line. See why ROAS guarantees are a red flag.
  • Reports in cost per customer. Clicks and impressions are inputs, not results.
  • Case studies you can check. Ask what the numbers were measured against. How to read agency case studies covers what to ask.
  • A clear first 90 days. Ask what changes in month one. The full list is in questions to ask a performance marketing agency.

Straight answers.

What does a performance marketing agency do?

It runs paid campaigns on platforms like Google and Meta and is judged on measurable results such as leads, sales and cost per customer. That covers campaign management, creative testing, conversion tracking and weekly reporting.

Is performance marketing the same as digital marketing?

Performance marketing is the part of digital marketing that is paid for and judged on a result. Digital marketing also includes organic social, SEO and brand work that is not tied to a cost per lead or sale.

What's your minimum ad spend?

There is a floor, and where it sits depends on your category and market. Below that an account can't generate enough conversion data to learn from, and the retainer takes too large a share of the total. If you're under that, we'll say so on the first call and tell you what to fix first instead.

How do you charge?

A monthly retainer, month to month, no lock-in. The fee depends on spend level and how many channels are running. We don't take a percentage of ad spend — it rewards us for spending more of your money, which is the wrong incentive.

How long before I see results?

Weeks one and two are audit, tracking and setup, with volatile numbers. The first honest read comes around week four. A fair judgement needs about 90 days, because that's how long it takes to find winning creative and scale it.

Do you guarantee a ROAS or a lead cost?

No. Anyone promising a number before seeing your margins, offer and historical data is guessing and building in an excuse. What we commit to is the work, a weekly report with decisions in it, and telling you early if it isn't going to work.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do, always. Accounts, pixel, data and creative files stay in your name. Agencies that keep the account are holding you hostage.

Will you tell me if ads aren't right for my business?

Yes, and we do regularly. If your offer, margins or follow-up can't support paid acquisition yet, that's the call you get on the first conversation — not after three months of retainers.
