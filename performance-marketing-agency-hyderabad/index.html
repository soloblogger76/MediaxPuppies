Hyderabad's paid market is dominated by real estate and healthcare, and both are categories where the enquiry count tells you almost nothing useful.

A plot or apartment enquiry at ₹100 is easy to generate and mostly worthless; the number that matters is cost per site visit. A hospital or clinic enquiry is similar — a form fill is not a patient, and optimising for form fills teaches the platform to find people who fill forms.

Healthcare here also runs into platform policy constantly. Before-and-after imagery and personal-attribute copy get accounts restricted, and the compliant version usually converts better anyway because it attracts people who trust you rather than people chasing a miracle.

We're based in Delhi NCR and work with Hyderabad clients remotely. Ad accounts don't need anyone in the room, and we'd rather be honest about that than claim an office we don't have. Weekly calls, WhatsApp in between, and we travel for anything that genuinely needs a face.
