Before you enquire

The questions we get asked every week.

How do you decide which of these a business actually needs?+

On the first call we work out two numbers: what a customer is worth to you, and how many you can handle a month. Those decide everything else. A business with capacity for ten more customers and a ₹1 lakh customer value needs paid ads and tracking, not an influencer programme. We would rather sell you one service that works than a bundle that looks impressive on an invoice.

Can I start with just one service?+

Yes, and most clients do. Performance marketing plus tracking is the usual starting point because it produces enquiries in weeks. SEO and AI automation get added once there is enough traffic and lead volume for them to pay back.

What is the minimum budget to work with you?+

An ad spend large enough for the platforms to learn from, paid directly to Meta or Google from your own account. Below that they do not get enough conversion data to optimise, so lead quality suffers and you would be paying us to manage a campaign that cannot learn. Our management fee is quoted separately once we know the scope.

Do you lock clients into a contract?+

No. Every engagement is month to month. You own the ad accounts, the pixel data, the audiences and anything we build, so leaving costs you nothing but notice.

How quickly will I see results?+

Paid campaigns typically go live 5–7 days after kickoff and produce first enquiries within 48–72 hours of launch, with the first fortnight spent calibrating. SEO is a 3–6 month horizon. AI automation shows up immediately in response time and in how many leads actually get followed up.

What reporting do I get?+

A weekly summary with spend, leads, cost per lead and what changed — in plain language, sent on WhatsApp. You also keep direct access to the ad accounts, so nothing we report is something you cannot verify yourself.

Engagements are month-to-month.

If the numbers stop working, you stop paying. That's the whole contract.
