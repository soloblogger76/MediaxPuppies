Straight answers.

Will this replace my sales team?

No, and it shouldn't. It gets a human into the conversation faster and better prepared. An automation that tries to close a sale in India gets clocked in about three messages, and the trust you lose costs more than the time you saved.

Do you pretend the bot is a person?

Never. The first message says it's an assistant. Indian buyers work it out quickly, and the discovery poisons everything after it.

How long does a build take?

Days, not quarters. The instant-reply and routing builds are usually live within a week. CRM sync and conversion feedback take longer depending on what you already use.

What does it cost to run?

A one-time build, plus the underlying tool costs — a WhatsApp Business API provider and a workflow tool. We'll tell you the running cost before you commit, and it's usually a few thousand rupees a month.

What if I don't have a CRM?

For under about 30 leads a month you probably don't need one — WhatsApp Business labels and a shared sheet genuinely work. Above that we'll recommend something simple, and the criterion is whether it captures WhatsApp conversations properly.

Which AI does it actually use?

Whatever fits the job. Drafting replies and summarising conversations benefits from a language model; routing and escalation are plain logic and don't need AI at all. We don't add a model where a rule would do.
