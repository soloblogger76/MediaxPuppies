The short answer

What is white label PPC?

White label PPC is an arrangement where a specialist team runs pay-per-click advertising, such as Google Ads and Facebook ads, for an agency’s clients under that agency’s name. The agency sells the service, owns the client relationship and sets the price. The white label partner does the account work and stays invisible: no logo, no contact with the client, reporting written in the agency’s template.

It differs from ordinary outsourcing in one way that matters. In outsourcing, the supplier is often visible and sometimes builds its own relationship with the end client. In white label PPC, the partner is contractually bound to stay behind the agency, which is why a non-solicit clause and an NDA come before any account access. How the whole model works in practice is covered in white label PPC, explained.

How it works

You stay in front. We stay behind.

The whole arrangement is designed so your client never has a reason to wonder who is doing the work — and so you never have to lie about it either.

Access without exposure

You add us as a partner on the client’s Business Manager or Google Ads account, or you give us a seat inside yours. Nothing we do carries our name. Where a platform shows a managing entity, it shows yours. If your client ever asks directly whether you use a delivery partner, tell them the truth — we will never contradict you, and we will never contact them.

Reporting in your template

We write into your reporting format, in your tone, on your schedule. If you use Looker Studio, we build in your workspace. If you send a written Monday note, you get a written Monday note you can forward with your name on it. What we will not do is send you a dashboard screenshot and call it a report — every report carries what changed, why, and what happens next.

One point of contact, in writing

You get a named buyer and a shared channel, not a ticket queue. Everything that happens to the account is logged, so when your client asks a question three weeks later you can answer it yourself without waiting on us.

NDA as standard, non-solicit both ways

A mutual NDA is signed before we see an account. We do not approach your clients, during the engagement or after it. We will also put in writing that we will not take on a direct competitor of a named client inside your account set if that matters to you.

The commercial case

Margin without headcount.

The reason agencies outsource media buying is rarely capability. It is that a good buyer is expensive, hard to hire, hard to keep busy, and catastrophic to lose at the wrong moment.

A per-account fee you can price against

You pay us a fixed monthly fee per account. You charge your client whatever your market supports. The spread is yours and it does not change when the account scales, because we do not take a percentage of ad spend — which also means we have no incentive to push your client’s budget up.

No bench to carry

A single in-house buyer is a fixed cost whether you have three accounts or eleven, and they cannot cover annual leave, illness or a resignation. Per-account pricing flexes with your book. Win four accounts in a quarter and we absorb them; lose two and your cost falls the same month.

Continuity through your own churn

When your buyer resigns, the institutional knowledge of every account walks out with them. This is the single most common reason agencies call us, and it is always urgent. Because our decision log lives with the account rather than with a person, handover is a document, not an archaeology project.

Scope

What we take, and what stays with you.

Clear lines from the start prevent the drift that kills these arrangements in month four.

We take the account execution

Account structure, campaign build, audience and keyword work, bidding and budget management, feed and catalogue setup, pixel and Conversions API implementation, conversion tracking, daily monitoring, disapproval handling and appeals, testing roadmap, and the weekly written report.

You keep the client and the brand

Strategy sign-off, brand voice, the client relationship, commercial terms, and final approval on creative concepts stay with you. We will propose angles and write ad copy; you decide what represents your client.

Creative production is a separate conversation

We can write copy and direct static and simple video concepts. Full production — shoots, motion design, heavy edit work — is not included by default. If you have a studio, we brief it. If you do not, say so early and we will scope it rather than quietly under-delivering on creative volume, which is the usual failure mode for a paid social account.

Before you sign

How to choose a white label PPC partner.

  • Non-solicit and NDA before access. If a partner hesitates, stop there.
  • The client’s accounts stay in the client’s name. A partner that wants to own the ad account is building leverage over you.
  • Reporting you can forward unchanged. Ask for a sample report in your template before the first account.
  • A fixed per-account fee, not a percentage of spend. A percentage rewards the partner for raising your client’s budget.
  • A trial on one account. The first audit of an account you already know tells you more than any pitch.

The questions agencies actually ask.

What is white label PPC?

A specialist team runs Google Ads, Facebook ads and other paid campaigns for an agency’s clients under the agency’s name. The agency keeps the client relationship and sets the price; the partner stays invisible.

Is white label PPC the same as outsourcing?

It is a form of outsourcing with one difference: the partner is contractually bound to stay behind your brand and never contact your client, so the relationship stays yours.

Do you run white label Facebook ads as well as Google Ads?

Yes. Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google Ads, including Performance Max and Shopping, are the core of the service. LinkedIn, TikTok and YouTube depend on the account.

How is white label PPC priced?

A fixed monthly fee per account, month to month, invoiced in your currency. We do not take a percentage of ad spend. The fee depends on the account and we say it on the first call.

Will you ever contact my client?

No. Not during the engagement, not after it ends. It is in the mutual NDA, and it is also simply how we want to operate — an outsourcing partner that poaches has one good quarter and no business after it.

Whose name is on the ad account?

Your client's, ideally, with you as the managing partner and us as a seat inside your access. We never hold the account ourselves. That is deliberate: it means you are never dependent on us to keep serving your own client.

What do I tell my client?

Whatever you are comfortable with. Plenty of partners say openly that they have a specialist media buying team; plenty do not mention it. We just need to know which, so we match it. We will never put you in the position of having been caught out.

Can I start with a single account?

Yes, and we recommend it. One live account, one audit, one month. You will learn more from reading our first audit of an account you already understand than from any pitch we could give you.

Do you ask for exclusivity or a minimum number of accounts?

Neither. No minimum, no exclusivity, month to month. If we are good the volume comes on its own, and if we are not, a lock-in only delays your decision.

How fast can you take over an account?

Access on day one, a written audit within two working days, and changes starting in week one unless we think the account needs the tracking fixed first. We will not rebuild a live account in the first 48 hours just to look busy — that destroys learning your client paid for.

Which platforms do you cover?

Meta, Google Ads including Performance Max and Shopping, and depending on the account TikTok, LinkedIn and YouTube. We will tell you when a platform is outside what we run well rather than take the account and learn on your client's budget.
