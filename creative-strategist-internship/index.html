Questions people ask before applying.

Is there any fee to apply or join?

No. We pay you, not the other way round. Any message asking for a registration fee, deposit or training charge in our name is a fraud. Please report it to us.

Do I need to be a professional designer?

No. You need to be able to make a basic ad end to end: a static in Canva or Figma, or a short edit in CapCut. The internship is about why an ad works, and the thinking matters more than polish.

I already have a year or two of experience. Should I apply here?

Apply here if you want a remote, part-time start. If you have run creative for paid campaigns before and want a full-time role, look at the Creative Strategist opening on the careers page instead.

Which tools do I need?

Canva or Figma for statics, CapCut or any video editor for Reels, and a laptop with a stable connection. We will show you how we use the Meta Ad Library and our testing sheet.

Will my creatives actually run?

The ones that pass review go into live tests on client accounts, and you see how they performed. Not every piece will run; that review is part of what you learn.

Is it really work from home?

Yes, fully remote, anywhere in India. Everything runs over WhatsApp, email and one weekly video call.
