Delhi's advertising market is broader and messier than Gurugram's. You have decades-old retail businesses running their first campaigns next to venture-funded D2C brands, and the two need completely different approaches.

The pattern we see most in Delhi accounts is a business with genuine demand and a broken funnel: real enquiries arriving, nobody calling them back inside the hour, and a landing page that was built for a desktop in 2019. Fixing the follow-up usually returns more than any campaign restructure.

Questions from Delhi businesses.

Are you actually based in Delhi?

Yes — Delhi NCR. We can meet in person, though most of the work happens over weekly calls and WhatsApp.

Do you handle local SEO as well as ads?

Yes. For any business with a catchment area we'd usually argue the Google Business Profile deserves attention before the ad budget goes up — it's free and it compounds.

What's your minimum ad spend?

There is a floor, and where it sits depends on your category and market. Below that the account can't learn and a retainer eats the budget. We'll tell you what to fix first instead.

My leads never answer the phone. Is that a targeting problem?

Almost never. It's usually the conversion event you're optimising for, or a follow-up gap. Spam calls have trained people not to answer unknown numbers — a WhatsApp message before the call changes contact rates immediately.
