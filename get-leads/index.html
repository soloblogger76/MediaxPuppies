The questions everyone asks

What does it cost?+

Two separate things. Your ad budget goes directly to Meta from your own card — we never touch it. Our management fee is quoted after the first call, once we know what we’re running. We won’t quote a number before understanding your business, and anyone who does is guessing.

How fast will leads start?+

Setup and tracking take about a week. Ads usually go live in 5–7 days and the first enquiries land within 48–72 hours of launch. The first two weeks are the campaign learning — cost per lead drops after that, not before.

What if the leads are rubbish?+

Some junk exists in every paid channel. Our job is to keep it low, which is why the qualifying questions sit before the form rather than after. We review lead quality with you weekly and tighten the filter until the ratio works for your sales team.

Do you guarantee results?+

No, and be careful with anyone who does. What we guarantee is transparent numbers, daily management, and a straight answer within 30 days on whether this channel works for your business.

Who owns the ad account?+

You do. Campaigns run inside your Meta account, billed to your card. If we part ways you keep the account, the pixel history and the audiences.

Is this different from buying leads from a portal?+

Completely. Portal leads are sold to four or five of your competitors at once. These come from your own ads and reach only you.
