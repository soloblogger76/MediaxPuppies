Common questions

Asked by almost every business owner we talk to.

How fast will I start getting leads?+

Typically 5–7 days from kickoff: setup and tracking first, then ads go live. First leads usually arrive within 48–72 hours of the campaign starting. The first 2 weeks are calibration — quality and cost improve as data builds.

What does it cost?+

Two parts: your ad budget (paid directly to Meta/Google from your own account — we never touch it) and our management fee. There is a minimum monthly ad budget below which the ads can't gather enough data to learn and lead quality suffers; where it sits depends on your category and city. We'll give you exact numbers on the first call, based on your customer value.

How is this different from Justdial or other lead sellers?+

Those platforms sell the same lead to 4–5 of your competitors. Leads we generate come from your own ads and land only with you — exclusive, fresh, and trackable back to the rupee spent.

What if the leads are junk?+

Some junk is unavoidable in any paid channel — our job is to keep it low with qualifying questions and continuous filtering. We review lead quality with you weekly and adjust targeting until the ratio works for your sales process.

How much budget does my industry actually need?+

Rough guide: interior design and clinics get usable data at a lower monthly spend. Real estate and solar are far more competitive and need materially more. The exact number depends on your city and your average customer value — we run that calculation with you on the first call.

Do you guarantee results?+

No honest agency guarantees a fixed number of customers — anyone who does is lying to you. What we guarantee: transparent numbers, daily management, and a straight answer within 30 days on whether this channel works for your business.
